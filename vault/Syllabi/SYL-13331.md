## Description
The Social Media Marketing Specialization is designed to achieve two objectives. It gives students the social analytics tools, and training to help them become an influencer on social media. The course also gives the knowledge and resources to build a complete social media marketing strategy – from consumer insights to final justification metrics.

## Learning Outcomes
| No. | CLO Name | CLO Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | CLO1 | Understand how to grow your social strategy using effective, proven methodologies. |
| 2 | CLO2 | Understand the Big Data of social and gain a more complete picture of what can be learned from interactions on social sites. |
| 3 | CLO3 | Understand two of the most effective social strategies used by organizations today to reach your target consumer markets, and know when it is best to either use or avoid specific social marketing strategies. |
| 4 | CLO4 | Understand a thoughtful, integrated approach to make content that stands out in our increasingly oversaturated world. |
| 5 | CLO5 | Understand how to transform your organization's social marketing from an untracked investment to an integral part of your company’s marketing strategy, and the legal considerations involved as well as proven performance metrics and management tactics for success. |
| 6 | CLO6 | Understand how to use methodologies, tools, and insights you have learned to create a multifaceted plan to assure effective social marketing as an integral part of your business strategy. |
