## Description
This capstone project course is designed to consolidate and apply students' knowledge in generative AI and large language models through a substantial team project. Students scope, design, build, evaluate, and present a non-trivial generative AI system or empirical study. Topics applied include: - The application of generative AI techniques (LLMs, diffusion, RAG, agents, fine-tuning) to solve practical problems. - The identification and adaptation of generative AI methodologies from current research for specific use cases. - The development of a comprehensive solution approach using prompt engineering, retrieval-augmented generation, fine-tuning (LoRA / QLoRA), or agent design. - The evaluation and analysis of generative outputs using appropriate metrics (perplexity, ROUGE, faithfulness, FID, human / LLM-as-judge protocols) and rigorous failure-mode analysis. - Recognition of appropriate scenarios for prompting vs RAG vs fine-tuning vs agent design. - Selection of suitable metrics and benchmarks for generative-system evaluation. - Understanding the ethical, safety, provenance, and legal implications of generative AI (hallucination, bias, deepfakes, copyright, content provenance / C2PA). - Implementation and reflection on generative pipelines including data preparation, model adaptation, inference / serving, and post-deployment monitoring. - Effective teamwork and project management across a multi-phase delivery.
