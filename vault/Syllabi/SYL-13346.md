## Description
The course equips students with basic knowledges of structure, human body proportions and practice drawing as well as human drawing. This is a foundation course for speedy drawing and animated design. Students will study the structure of the body's anatomy, then apply the gained knowledge to perform the drawing on human model. Each human anatomy and drawing study lasts for six slots and is performed in the classroom, under the guidance of the instructor. Anatomical research, student will have to carry at home on basis of the model drawing lesson on the class. The final class of the sixth slot will be scored. Lecturers and students make a mutual assessment, lecturer gives final score and will be counted as the credit mark.

| 3 | CLO3 | Analyze the Human in Action, then apply that learned skill to the practice of drawing human in action. |
| 4 | CLO4 | Explain the bones and muscles structure that lie beneath the skin. Analize the structure of the human body that lie beneath the clothes. |
| 5 | CLO5 | Have a positive attitude and a professional working style. |
