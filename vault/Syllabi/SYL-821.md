## Description
By the end of this course Students will be able to: 1. Understand the purpose of packaging design 2. Create effective and innovative package design solutions. 3. Utilize effective packaging principles and techniques to influence consumer choice 4. Understand the form and function requirements that packaging must, enclose, inform and persuade. 6. Evaluate effective packaging methods and processes 7. Implement recommendations from focus groups in order to understand consumer behaviour and create a better 8. Collaborate within a group 9. Prepare professional presentations from initial phase research to a full comprehensive package. 10. Be conscious of sustainable design issues
