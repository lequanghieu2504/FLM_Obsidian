## Description
* Describes basic business function area and explain how they are related. * Illustrates how unintergrated information system fail to support business functions and business processes that cut across functional area boundaries. * Demonstrated how intergrated information system can help a company prosper by improving business processes and by providing business managerswith accurate, consistent, and current data.
