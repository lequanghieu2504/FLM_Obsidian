## Description
This course based on the Deep Learning Specialization at https://www.coursera.org/specializations/deep-learning. This break into AI by Deeplearning. Deep Learning is a superpower. With it you can make a computer see, synthesize novel art, translate languages, render a medical diagnosis, or build pieces of a car that can drive itself. If that isn’t a superpower, I don’t know what is. — Andrew Ng, Founder of deeplearning.ai and Coursera
