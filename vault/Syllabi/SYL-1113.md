## Description
Reading skill Upon completion of the course, students should be able to: - Guess meaning from context - Make inferences - Indentify main ideas and supporting ideas through skimming and scanning - Distinguish facts from opinion - Identify pronoun references - Understand text organization - Understand patterns of organization - Understand figurative language - Recognize oganizational phrases - Take notes - Summarize - Understand paragraph transitions - Paraphrase -
