## Description
*Description: English Academic Writing provides a systematic introduction to the principles and conventions of academic writing. The course offers a thorough analysis of common academic writing tasks, focusing on paragraph and essay development. Students will learn to plan, draft, revise, and edit their writing effectively, producing coherent and well-structured academic texts. *Objectives: By the end of the course, students will be able to: - Analyze the organization and structure of academic paragraphs and essays. - Understand and apply the conventions and norms of academic writing. - Develop critical thinking, analytical, and reasoning skills to construct well-supported arguments. - Produce different genres of academic writing, including cause/effect, process, comparison/contrast, argumentative, problem/solution, and classification essays. *Teaching methods: - The course adopts a flexible, student-centered approach. Instructors may select teaching methods that best suit their class context, such as using lecture slides, guided learning materials, and interactive activities. Lessons incorporate pair work, small-group, and whole-class discussions to engage students in all stages of the writing process—planning, drafting, revising, editing, and evaluating written work. - AI-based writing tools (e.g., ChatGPT, Grammarly, QuillBot) may be integrated to support brainstorming, revising, and self-evaluating students’ writing. The use of AI is guided by principles of academic integrity and responsible learning to enhance writing autonomy and reflection.

| 1 | CLO1 | Identify and analyze the purposes, structures, and composition of various academic writing styles and forms. (Lectures, class discussions, and guided analysis of model essays using AI text-analysis tools (e.g., ChatGPT or Gemini) to identify patterns in organization and language.) |
| 2 | CLO2 | Develop and organize coherent academic paragraphs with clear topic sentences, supporting ideas, and logical progression. (Brainstorming and outlining ideas using AI tools (ChatGPT, Grammarly, Quillbot) to generate and organize topic sentences and supporting points; peer review through AI feedback suggestions.) |
| 3 | CLO3 | Apply appropriate academic vocabulary, grammar, and sentence structures to produce accurate and cohesive writing. (Grammar and vocabulary exercises supported by AI-based checkers (Grammarly, Gemini) and teacher-led correction sessions.) |
| 4 | CLO4 | Integrate and cite external sources effectively through summarizing, paraphrasing, and quoting in line with academic conventions. (Practice paraphrasing and citation using AI paraphrasing/citation tools (Quillbot, Turnitin) and group tasks to evaluate AI-generated outputs for accuracy.) |
| 5 | CLO5 | Produce well-structured academic essays that demonstrate unity, coherence, and logical argumentation. (Drafting and revising essays with AI-supported feedback (ChatGPT, Grammarly) followed by instructor evaluation and peer feedback sessions.) |
| 6 | CLO6 | Demonstrate ethics, professionalism, and responsible use of AI tools in academic writing and peer review. (Discussion and reflection on academic integrity, proper citation, and responsible AI use in writing; case studies and group debates.) |
