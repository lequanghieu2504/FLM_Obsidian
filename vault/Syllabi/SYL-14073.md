## Description
'- Objective: The course aims to provide students a solid foundation on different aspects of advertising and other major integrated marketing communications tools, and the role of these tools in the marketing process. - Description: In particular, attention will be given to discussions on (1) understand basic marketing communication and advertising related terms (2) understanding the communication processes, (3) understand creative process and creative choice (4) conducting research materials for creative work, (5) devising effective creative strategies for achieving marketing communication objectives, and (6) implementing and evaluating the creative strategy.
