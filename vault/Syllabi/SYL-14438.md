## Description
This course is designed to further develop the learner's confidence and fluency in using the English language and to enrich their learning experience through interactive lessons and communicative exercises. Upon completion of the course, students should be able to: - Identify main ideas and supporting details. recognize and apply organizational structures; - Demonstrate improved reading fluency by reading academic passages, and use contextual strategies (e.g., context clues, root word analysis, syntactic parsing) to interpret ambiguous passages in reading assignments. - Analyze the structure and content of peer-reviewed research articles by identifying and summarizing the research question, methodology, key findings, and implications in research articles.- Identify fand understand statements of acts and opinions, identify implication and inference; - Understand cause-and-effect relationships, understand causation and correlation; - Determine Author's purpose and tone; Interpret and explain information presented in visuals, charts, and graphs clearly and accurately; - Organize and structure ideas effectively at the sentence, paragraph, and essay levels in academic writing; - Deliver clear, well-organized, and impactful oral presentations with appropriate academic register and confidence; and - Master (280) academic vocabulary words from the Academic Word List, demonstrating accuracy on vocabulary assessments and correctly using target words in their written assignments and oral presentations. Students are also expect to: - Be cooperative, supportive, and responsible in group work - Take advantages of AI, online applications, language labs, and online platforms in performing learning tasks - Adhere to plagiarism rules in performing learning tasks. - Demonstrate critical thinking
