## Description
This course provides knowledge of Design History: The milestones of achievements in Design History with understanding of contexts; The relationship between design history and the development of technical capabilities, materials, and other social issues; The information of representative designers, characteristics of design styles and movements. This course also provides some basic skills such as research methods, critical thinking, and mind mapping in the study of design history. In addition, this subject requires teamwork skills, oral presentation and writing skills in the process of assessment.

## Learning Outcomes

| 3 | CLO3 | (Critical Thinking) Analyze and evaluate critically the records of the past in Design History. |
| 4 | CLO4 | (Communication Skills): Study optional cases in Design History, organize and express the individual thoughts clearly and coherently both in orally (presentations) and writing (assignments/essays). |
