## Description
- The course helps students grasp the basic knowledge to build up UI screen of Web and Mobile application. Beside understanding the historical, context, requirements, the evolution, trending and challenges of graphical UI design; throughout the systematic theories and practical exercises in class will help students understand and applied the techniques and supporting tools to solve the problem of interface design websites and mobile apps. - The result of pratical exercises will be analyzed, commented, and evaluated on class by lecturer and feedback from these other students. - During the course, students are guided to collect and arrange the result step-by-step into Portfolio. - The mobile UI design will be demontrated on the Android platform.
