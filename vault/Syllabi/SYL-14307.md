## Description
This course is derived from multiple MOOC on Coursera 1. Digital Transformation by Illinois Tech This course has 9 modules. Each offers a different perspectives on digital technology in the strategic context. It is designed for leaders focused on implementing new ideas, staying ahead of the competition and aligning their people, data and technology to drive digital transformation. First, we discuss the pace of change, and its impact, implications and opportunities. Traditional businesses need to rethink their underlying assumptions to create new game plans and capture new opportunities in the digital world. This course not only discusses key digital transformation strategies, trends and impacts, but also spends time on how to integrate these into your existing business and execute on those new game plans. 2. Google Cloud Digital Leader Training Professional Certificate The Cloud Digital Leader training offers a series of 6 courses that will lay a solid foundation for understanding cloud technology, data, AI/ML, and how these play a pivotal role in digital transformation through Google Cloud products and services. This program can prepare you to take the Google Cloud Digital Leader certification exam administered by Google Cloud. This program is designed to empower you and your team(s) to effectively participate in and contribute to cloud-centric business efforts across your organization. It is ideal for IT professionals, data scientists, software engineers, project managers, decision-makers, leaders, and those seeking the certification. The program explores various core areas, including general cloud and Google Cloud know-how, modernizing company IT infrastructure and applications, and evoking digital transformation through data and AI/ML. Upon completion, you will be able to recall critical cloud terminologies, recognize Google Cloud products and solutions driving digital transformation, discuss how cloud technology and data can steer innovation within organizations, and identify key changes to leverage Google Cloud products for infrastructure and application modernization. Applied Learning Project Validate the newly acquired knowledge through practice and graded assessments at the end of each module. These assessments validate and demonstrate your grasp of the desired learning outcomes.
