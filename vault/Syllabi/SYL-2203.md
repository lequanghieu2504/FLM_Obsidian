## Description
The course is designed to enable students to: - develope English language and communicate skills for the workplace, do business, cross-cultural understanding, and business case study analysis - apply these skills in the final Capstone Project by analysing a current business case study and providing recommendations to a target audience in both presentation and written formats. 1) Ongoing assessment: Student gets 0.5 bonus points for each course completed on time. The total bonus point is not greater than 1. 2) Practical Exam (PE) 3) Final Result (FR) =min (10, PE+ Bonus) 4) Completion Criteria: PE>=4 and FR>=5

## Learning Outcomes
| No. | CLO Name | CLO Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | CLO1 | Utilize English language in delivering effective job interviews; giving persuasive business pitches, delivering informative business presentations, speaking persuasively in English, adapting content to purpose, context and audience, and using appropriate style and tone of writing for business purposes |
| 2 | CLO2 | Utilize English in identifying possible cross-cultural issues and misunderstandings in communication; applying strategies to overcome possible cross-cultural issues and misunderstandings in communication; producing documents with an appropriate style for business communication purposes within a cross-cultural situation; and delivering a speech with an appropriate style for business communication purposes within a cross-cultural situation |
