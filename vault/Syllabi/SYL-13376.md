## Description
This course aimed to provide students knowledge related to Interaction and UX Design in mobility world including smartphone devices and other wearable ones. In this course, student will be taught the common principles which dominates on mobile interface design in general. Specified principles corresponding three popular mobile platforms such as Android, iOS and Windows are going to be discussed, too. An agile for researching and designing Interaction and UX in mobility will also be introduced and applied through out this course. At the end of this course, student will obtain practical experience via a three phase projects in which they have to apply learned principles. A high fidelity prototype tools and paper prototype techniques will be explained in this course.

## Learning Outcomes
| No. | CLO Name | CLO Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | CLO1 | Explain the core principles of interaction and user experience (UX) design in mobile and wearable contexts, including smartphones, smartwatches, and other responsive platforms. |
| 2 | CLO2 | Identify and analyze platform-specific UI/UX guidelines (e.g., Android, iOS, and others) and adapt design approaches accordingly. |
| 3 | CLO3 | Conduct UX and visual research to gather user needs, identify design patterns, and explore visual languages that inform concept development and design directions. |
| 4 | CLO4 | Apply the obtained knowledge to implement a research and designing process for a real product. |
| 5 | CLO5 | Investigate and analyze technologies relevant to the product context (e.g., APIs, sensors, databases), and propose practical solutions that define how data is processed and how user actions flow through the system. |
| 6 | CLO6 | Design and prototype digital products by expressing distinctive visual concepts through both low- and high-fidelity prototypes using up-to-date design tools and systems. |
| 7 | CLO7 | Evaluate user experience through usability testing and user feedback, and refine solutions through improvement cycles, focusing on consistency, performance, and emotional impact. |
