## Description
The course is designed to enable students to: - communicate effectively in English in professional contexts. - expand their English vocabulary, improve your ability to write and speak in English in both social and professional interactions, and learn terminology and skills which can be applied to business negotiations, telephone conversations, written reports and emails, and professional presentations. 1) Ongoing assessment: Student gets 0.5 bonus points for each course completed on time. The total bonus point is not greater than 1. 2) Practical Exam (PE) 3) Final Result (FR) =min (10, PE+ Bonus) 4) Completion Criteria: PE>=4 and FR>=5
