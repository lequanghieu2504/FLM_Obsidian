## Description
The course is designed to enable students to: - develop and deliver compelling presentations. - balance proven ideas with lots of speech practice drawn from the established knowledge in communication, rhetoric, linguistics, and argumentation, each course 1) Ongoing assessment: Student gets 0.5 bonus points for each course completed on time. The total bonus point is not greater than 1. 2) Practical Exam (PE) 3) Final Result (FR) =min (10, PE+ Bonus) 4) Completion Criteria: PE>=4 and FR>=5

## Learning Outcomes
| No. | CLO Name | CLO Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | CLO1 | Analyse factors of motivation among workplace counterparts; Recognise the impact of culture on factors of motivation; Critique and craft persuasive verbal communication products (i.e. script of dialogue) for exchanges with work teams and critical stakeholders; Apply principles of effective PowerPoint slide design with consideration for cultural sensitivity; Identify and adopt strategies on the use of voice and body language for engaging presentations. |
| 2 | CLO2 | Make a handbook or a practical guide, which could be a blend of text and multi-media, using and/or collecting relevant primary data from people in the workplace through emails, face-to-face chats, Skype discussions and incorporate the data into the handbook/guide as support. |
