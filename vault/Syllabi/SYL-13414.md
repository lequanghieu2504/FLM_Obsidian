## Description
This course explores the central concepts of organisational procurement, global sourcing and interfaces of these to the other areas of an organisation. This course provides opportunities to examine issues such as organisational procurement process, global sourcing process, supplier selection process, supplier management and other strategic issues. Teaching method: project-based learning. Students are required to use AI tools to conduct a supply market analysis for a selected industry. The task begins with a macro-level PESTLE analysis, followed by refining prompts to perform a Porter’s Five Forces analysis and identify specific sourcing risks. Throughout the process, students must optimize their use of AI by moving from general to detailed prompts in order to generate deeper insights. They are also expected to observe how different prompt structures influence the quality of strategic analysis, and to validate AI-generated results by cross-checking them against credible articles and industry reports.
