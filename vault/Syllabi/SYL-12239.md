## Description
The course includes two parts. - The first part covers a sufficient understanding of fundamental physics concepts about electromagnetism. This part explores the principles of electromagnetism, such as electromagnetic field, current, potential, capacitance, resistance, circuit... - The second part of this course aims to provide a general understanding of semiconductor devices. This part explores the principles and the operation mechanism of semiconductor, such as charge transfer, p-n junction, junction capacitors, and Metal-Oxide-Semiconductor Field Effect Transistors(MOSFETs), the Metal-Semiconductor Contact, Metal-Oxide-Semiconductor (MOS) capapcitor, CMOS, Metal-Semiconductor Field Effect Transistors (MESFETs), Memory and Bipolar Junction Transistor (BJT) to improve the overall knowledge of semiconductor industry."
