## Description
Writing Research Papers from essay to research paper develops learners' academic writing sklls offering: -review and extenstion of the typical parts and formats of essays -a clear guide to choosing and researching topics -step-by-step practice of the drafting process, using acade,ic language and presenting research -special sections on citing references and avoiding plagiarism

## Learning Outcomes
| No. | CLO Name | CLO Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | CLO1 | Describe the steps of the process of essay writing |
| 2 | CLO2 | Differenciate two types of research papers: persuasive and expository, practice to write a thesis statement and an essay outline. |
| 3 | CLO3 | Report the importance of introductions, conclusions and peer review. |
| 4 | CLO4 | Select online resources to determine which are the the best to use. |
| 5 | CLO5 | Create a detailed tree outline for the research paper. |
| 6 | CLO6 | Recognize the importance of crediting sources, and two strategies to avoid plagiarizing: quotating and paraphrasing |
| 7 | CLO7 | Use academic language in terms of style and tone in the research paper |
| 8 | CLO8 | Apply APA style for in-text citations |
| 9 | CLO9 | Write an abstract for the research paper |
| 10 | CLO | Write a research paper (~1000 words) |
