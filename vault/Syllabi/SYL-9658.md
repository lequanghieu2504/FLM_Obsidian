## Description
-Objective: To apply the theoretical knowledge to F&B practical activities -Description: This course is the continued part of RES213. It focuses on how to mix and serve wines and drinks. -Teaching method: demonstration, role play, group work

## Learning Outcomes
| No. | CLO Name | CLO Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | CLO1 | Explain the Stirring, Shaking, Blending, and Building methods of drink mixing. |
| 2 | CLO2 | Develop a sense of good discipline, a sense of responsibility, and a sense of serious work in the assigned work as well as with colleagues. |
| 3 | CLO3 | Develop ability to work quietly; with cooperation; patience, carefulness, cleanliness and aesthetic values. |
