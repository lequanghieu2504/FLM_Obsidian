## Description
The module equips you with knowledge on how to simulate and model elements in the automotive industry. In this course, students will learn about building digital models to simulate and test various elements of the automotive development process. This course provides fundamental concepts and practical techniques of modeling and simulation in the context of digital automotive systems. Students will learn to develop and analyze digital models to simulate vehicle subsystems, embedded software behavior, and automotive development processes, supporting design validation, system integration, and performance optimization in software-defined vehicles.
