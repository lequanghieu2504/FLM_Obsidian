## Description
This course aims at shaping students' effective communication skills in professional contexts using English. The course helps students expand their vocabulary, improve both speaking and writing skills in English, and learn terminology and skills which can be applied in different business situations and contexts. *Assessment scheme: 1) Ongoing assessment: Students who complete all MOOCs before the course deadline will receive 1 bonus point; otherwise, no bonus point will be awarded. 2) Theoretical Exam (TE) 3) Final Result (FR) =min (10, TE+ Bonus) 4) Completion Criteria: TE>=4 and FR>=5
