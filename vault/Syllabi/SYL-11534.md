## Description
This course covering basis programming with ABAP, high-level programming language developed by SAP, primarily used for building and customizing business applications within the SAP ecosystem. The students will learn from the basis (ABAP's syntax, data types, control structures, object-oriented programming) to advanced concepts (modulization, performance optimization...) of ABAP, ensuring a solid foundation and practical expertise ... Through interactive exercises, and hands-on projects, students will get familiar with developing SAP application in real-world scenarios.
