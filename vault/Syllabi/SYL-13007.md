## Description
In this course, students are introduced to a comprehensive 14-step process designed to address real-world, human-centered challenges of their own selection. Through a project-based, experiential learning approach, students will develop and demonstrate proficiency in the core competencies of Design Thinking. The curriculum emphasizes the application of key human-centered design tools to facilitate idea generation and foster meaningful engagement with end-users. Furthermore, students will critically engage with six fundamental phases of the design thinking process, examining the associated shifts in cognitive frameworks and skill development required at each stage.
