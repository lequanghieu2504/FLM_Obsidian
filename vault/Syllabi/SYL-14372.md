## Description
-Main objective: This course equips students with: (1) Understanding the Regulatory and Conceptual Frameworks (2) Practical Preparation of Financial Statements (3) Advanced Group Accounting and Consolidation (4) Analysis and Interpretation of Financial Data (5) Specialized and Simplified Reporting -Description: Students will master the conceptual and regulatory frameworks of IFRS alongside the practical skills needed to prepare individual and consolidated financial statements.They will develop technical expertise in applying standards to complex areas including revenue recognition, taxation, and group accounting. Furthermore, learners acquire the ability to evaluate an entity's financial health and investment potential by synthesizing cash flow trends, earnings metrics, and segmental performance to support economic decision-making. Finally, students gain insight into specialized reporting environments, including hyperinflationary economies and the IFRS for SMEs standard. -Teaching method: The course applies diverse pedagogical approaches such as lectures, analytical discussions, presentation skills, collaborative group work, case studies, problem-based learning. Assessment is integrated through applied exercises, situational analysis, and teamwork performance.
