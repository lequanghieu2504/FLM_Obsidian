## Description
"-Objective: Develop students' competence in applying linguistic and specific knowledge for handling translation of ESP documents. -Description: The course aims to delve into the translating practice of specialized documents in various fields. In addition to assisting learners to build up their own specialized glossaries, techniques and common specific knowledge, the course focuses on the translating practice of divergent documents covering such fields as literature, technology/IT, Legal/politics, medicine, and so on. The course can be implemented in the forms of projects, exhibits or workshop, and so on. -Teaching method: Communicative approach
