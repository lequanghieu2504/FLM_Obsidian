## Description
Create a digital product follow the design process: research, empathize with users, identify pain points, finding solutions, create wireframes and prototype, test and iterate on designs. Practice UX research: like planning research studies, conducting interviews and usability studies, and synthesizing research results Apply foundational UX concepts, like user-centered design, accessibility, and equity-focused design - Students must work in a group. The number of members for each group, decided by the Head Academic of campus, could be variable from 2 to 3 students - The project is done in 4 stages with feedback and discussion at each stage. Students are expected to discuss, organise, present and critique their work with a high level of professionalism, and implement research strategies that contribute to the overall finished project 1) Progress on Project (04 reports): 45% - Report 1: 15% - Report 2: 10% - Report 3: 10% - Report 4: 10% Mark of each report must be >= 4/10. 2) Final Documents (By Instructor): 5% Final Documents (By Instructor) >=4 3) Project Defense - Final Documents (By Council): 20%; - Oral Project Defense: 30% Project Defense >=4 4) Final results:100%; Final Result >=5/10 Final Products and Final Documents must be submitted before deadline

## Learning Outcomes
| No. | CLO Name | CLO Details |

| 1 | CLO1 | Identify and frame a user experience problem within a specific domain by applying relevant theoretical frameworks and research methods, while demonstrating awareness of social responsibilities and compliance with legal, ethical, and contextual constraints. |
| 2 | CLO2 | Analyze user behaviors, needs, motivations, and frustrations, along with stakeholder perspectives, using synthesis methods such as empathy mapping, persona building, and user journey mapping. |
| 3 | CLO3 | Generate interface design concepts through information architecture, wireframing that respond to the defined problem and align with user goals and technical feasibility. |
| 4 | CLO4 | Build interactive prototypes using digital tools (e.g., Figma), supported by a finalized UI design and visual system (including logo and branding guidelines) that clearly express the product identity. |
| 5 | CLO5 | Conduct usability testing or heuristic evaluation, analyze feedback, and iteratively refine the product to improve usability, consistency, and alignment with user expectations. |

| 7 | CLO7 | Present the project solution through a structured design document, visual communication (poster, slides), oral presentation and defend with clarity and professionalism. |
| 8 | CLO8 | Evaluate critically and integrate ethically integrate sources and tools—including generative AI—by reflecting on their credibility, limitations, and ethical implications. Propose responsible approaches to maintain sustainability and uphold human values. |
