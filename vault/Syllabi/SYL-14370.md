## Description
This course equips learners with advanced English skills for academic, social, and professional communication. The course develops mastery of complex grammar, vocabulary, and pronunciation while fostering critical and cross-cultural awareness. Students analyze and discuss global and ethical issues, comprehend extended spoken and written texts, and express opinions or arguments with fluency and accuracy. Emphasis is placed on essay writing, critical reading, and effective collaboration. Learners integrate technology and AI tools for research and self-assessment, promoting leadership, professionalism, and lifelong global engagement.
