## Description
The Capstone Project is an integrative experience that brings together various components of the undergraduate AI studies in a more applied setting and under a new perspective. It enables students to further apply their knowledge and skills which they have found especially interesting or beneficial to them. As part of the course, students will learn about all aspects of the process of developing and carrying out the capstone project and gain an understanding of standards and expectations needed to successfully complete their dissertation. This capstone project will be completed by a group of 2-3 students. Students will be supported by a supervisor, who will provide them with essential guidance in conducting a research, designing & implementing the product, writing the dissertation in formal format and defending their arguments in front of the examination board.
