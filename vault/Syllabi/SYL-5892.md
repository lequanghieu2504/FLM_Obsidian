## Description
The Graduation Thesis is an integrative experience that brings together various components of the undergraduate computer science studies in a more applied setting and under a new perspective. It enables students to further their knowledge and skills which they have found to be especially interesting or beneficial to them. As part of the course, students will learn about all aspects of the process of developing and carrying out the graduation thesis and gain an understanding of standards and expectations needed to successfully complete their dissertation. This thesis project will be completed by a group of 2-3 computer science students. Each thesis of student(s) will be supported by a supervisor, who will provide them with essential guidance in conducting a research, writing a thesis in a formal format and defending their arguments in front of the examination board.
