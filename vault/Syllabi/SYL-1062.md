## Description
The course covers a sufficient understanding of modern physics concepts, and helps students apply this conceptual understanding to quantitative problem solving. It also helps students to perceive underlying physics phenomena through practical home projects. Topic covered: Special Relativity, Quantum Mechanics and Quantum Computing.
