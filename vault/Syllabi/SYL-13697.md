## Description
This course provides students with the knowledge and experience necessary for success in the entertainment industry. It comprises two parts: First, "Entertainment Business Foundations," which focuses on the music industry, provides a comprehensive overview, including the industry's history and role dynamics. In the second section, you'll explore the intersection of fandom, popular culture, and digital media, learning how to analyse popular culture products, map fan networks, and understand the links between popular culture and the political and commercial factors that shape its production, distribution, and consumption.
