## Description
GRC490 is an integrative capstone experience bridging Business Administration and Software Engineering. Interdisciplinary teams identify a genuine market problem, validate the business opportunity, design and build a functioning, deployable digital product, and defend both its business viability and technical implementation. Each team is guided by two co-supervisors—one from each faculty—and each student is assessed individually before a mixed committee.

## Learning Outcomes
| No. | CLO Name | CLO Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | CLO1 | Demonstrate an ability to integrate cross-disciplinary knowledge (business and technology), handle complexity, and plan and carry out advanced tasks independently and as a group member within specified time limits. |
| 2 | CLO2 | Use appropriate market research methodology to identify market problems, validate demand, and propose a digital solution that is both technically feasible and commercially viable. |
| 3 | CLO3 | Design, implement, and test a software system aligned with stated business requirements, using sound software engineering principles: requirements, architecture, iterative development, and testing. |
| 4 | CLO4 | Develop critical thinking, creative problem-solving, and management capabilities; analyze marketing, operational, and financial aspects of the digital product in theory and practice. |
| 5 | CLO5 | Apply project-management principles to coordinate an interdisciplinary team and deliver a working digital product within schedule and quality constraints. |
| 6 | CLO6 | Write, present and defend the thesis—including the business case and technical design—according to well-defined academic and professional criteria, with correct referencing. |
| 7 | CLO7 | Produce a feasible, deployable digital product or near-production MVP that demonstrates real-world entrepreneurial and engineering value and supports actual or potential business operation. |
