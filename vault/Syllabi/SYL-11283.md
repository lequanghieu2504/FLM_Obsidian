## Description
- This course satisfies the Graphic Design Faculty component of FPT University. Students will engage with technical, practical, and concept based problems involved with the practice of watercolor painting. Color theory, techniques, materials, and application will be explored throughout the term. Projects will include painting from the still life, model, landscape and images. - This course presents students with the basic techniques and concepts that are common to various painting media, with the emphasis placed on the watercolor medium. students in watercolor course will be exposed to both subjectively and technically. This course will also examine the interdependence of medium and image. Further more the course will provide basic knowledge of color application in visual design. - This course will be organized in a studio. During class time, practice exercises will come right after each of theories. Students create art works after lectures and tutorials. These art works will be analyzed and evaluated by students under the control of lecturer. Finally, they will be graded by the lecturer. - Students collect all of their art works to make the portfolios.
