## Description
Description: The module equips students with knowledge about tourist destinations within Vietnam such as geographical distribution, social, economic, cultural, and tourism development of leading source 7 tourism regions. From a geographical point of view, based on the classification of tourism resources, the countries of origin of tourists, or generating areas; and the routes traveled between these two sets of locations teaches students how to design, develop and evaluate a tour itinerary. Effectively apply AI to the course. Objectives This course helps students to master the characteristics of regions and the connections between them, from which students apply this knowledge to design appropriate travel itineraries and to present them in a convincing, attractive way to tourists/customers. Teaching Method: The course is designed based on the blended learning method. Students are required to read slides before coming to class. In class, the lecturer recalls the lesson and organizes discussion activities. The course includes a lot of practical sessions for interpreting and building travel itineraries.
