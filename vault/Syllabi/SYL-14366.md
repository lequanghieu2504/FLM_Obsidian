## Description
This beginner English foundation course provides beginner learners with essential English skills for everyday communication. It focuses on developing basic vocabulary, grammar, and pronunciation for real-life situations such as greetings, daily routines, and personal information. Students practice listening, speaking, reading, and writing through interactive and contextualized tasks. The course also fosters the ability to use technology and AI tools to support language learning, encourages collaboration and responsibility, and promotes self-assessment for continuous improvement and lifelong learning in a global context.
