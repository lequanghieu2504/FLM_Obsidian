## Description
-This course introduces students to a professional studio experience where their goal is to create a 3D animated short film from scratch using Autodesk Maya. -Students will be responsible for all aspects of production, from developing their own original story, creating storyboards and concept art to finalizing textures, animation, character models, and lighting in a complete short film.

## Learning Outcomes
| No. | CLO Name | CLO Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | CLO1 | Coming up with an idea that is suitable to create a short 3D animated. |
| 2 | CLO2 | Writing the script. Students will discover a lot when they translate the idea into written words. |
| 3 | CLO3 | Creating storyboard to pre-visualize the shorts. |
| 4 | CLO4 | Shoot live-action reference. It’s good practice to record yourself on video doing what your 3D character acts in your film. |
| 5 | CLO5 | Apply 3D skills such as Modeling, Texturing, Rigging to prepare ready characters for animation. |
| 6 | CLO6 | Apply animating workflow to animate the entire film. |
| 7 | CLO7 | Shading, lighting and rendering to establish the looks and feels of the film. |
| 8 | CLO8 | Adding sounds and editing sequences to create final film. |
| 9 | CLO9 | Students have a proficient in presentation, teamworks and evaluation products. Having a positive attitude and a professional working style |
| 10 | CLO10 | Be able to organize and implement Digital art & design projects effectively. |
