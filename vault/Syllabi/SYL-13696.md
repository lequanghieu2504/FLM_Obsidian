## Description
In this program, students are introduced to the foundations of project management through a hands-on, practice-based curriculum developed by Google experts. Over seven courses, students will explore both traditional and Agile approaches, gaining skills in risk management, budgeting, stakeholder communication, and team coordination. With more than 140 hours of interactive learning and real-world simulations, the program emphasizes practical application of project management tools and methods. Upon completion, students will be prepared for entry-level roles in project management and will be equipped with industry-recognized training that can lead to globally valued certifications and diverse career opportunities.

| 3 | CLO3 | Students will be able to analyze and address project challenges, including change management, team conflicts, and stakeholder engagement, and propose appropriate solutions. |
| 4 | CLO4 | Students will be able to evaluate and design project management solutions using modern tools and methodologies (Agile/Scrum), and propose optimized approaches to improve project performance and success. |
