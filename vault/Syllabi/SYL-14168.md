## Description
This course introduces the fundamental principles of robotic mechanical manipulation, focusing on the mathematics and control theories that govern how robots move and interact with their environment. Students will learn spatial descriptions and transformations in 3D space, forward and inverse manipulator kinematics, velocity and force analysis using Jacobians, manipulator dynamics, and trajectory generation. The course also covers linear control strategies for achieving desired motion performance along planned trajectories. Through progressive programming assignments and MATLAB-based simulations, students gain hands-on experience applying theoretical concepts to practical manipulator problems. This foundational knowledge prepares students for advanced topics in autonomous systems, intelligent robotics, and real-world robotic application development.

## Learning Outcomes
| No. | CLO Name | CLO Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | CLO1 | Understand Robotics and Manipulation Fundamentals: Students can describe the basic concepts of robotic mechanical manipulation, including robot types, coordinate frames, spatial descriptions, and homogeneous transformations in 3D space. |
| 2 | CLO2 | Analyze Manipulator Kinematics: Students can apply forward and inverse kinematics methods using the Denavit–Hartenberg convention to determine the position and orientation of robotic manipulators. |
| 3 | CLO3 | Evaluate Velocity, Force, and Dynamics: Students can utilize Jacobian-based analysis to relate joint velocities and static forces, and formulate the dynamic equations of motion governing manipulator behavior. |
| 4 | CLO4 | Design and Plan Robot Trajectories: Students can generate and evaluate smooth joint-space and Cartesian-space trajectories for robotic manipulators based on motion planning principles. |
| 5 | CLO5 | Apply Linear Control Strategies: Students can design and analyze linear control schemes to achieve stable and accurate trajectory tracking performance for robotic manipulators. |
| 6 | CLO6 | Implement and Simulate Manipulator Systems: Students can develop and test manipulator software through progressive programming assignments and simulation tool, applying theoretical concepts to practical robotic problems. |
