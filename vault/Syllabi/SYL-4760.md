## Description
The course is designed to enable students to: - Apply language in introducing oneself, one's job or one's preferences - Utilize language to start a conversation and communicate with someone new form other countries - Indicate one's opinion about family, friendship, hometown, or jobs 1) Ongoing assessment: Student gets 0.25 bonus points for each course completed on time. The total bonus point is not greater than 1. 2) Practical Exam (PE) 3) Final Result (FR) =min (10, PE+ Bonus) 4) Completion Criteria: PE>=4 and FR>=5
