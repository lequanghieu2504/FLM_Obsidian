## Description
This course equips students with foundational and practical knowledge of international trade, viewed through the lens of supply chain and logistics management. It explores the roles of key stakeholders in global trade, the interplay between international trade and freight forwarding, and the structure of trade contracts. Students will examine critical elements such as Incoterms 2020, delivery and payment terms, and common risks in cross-border transactions. The course also covers international trade regulations, major institutions governing global commerce, and financial and legal frameworks, including trade financing and dispute resolution mechanisms. Through hands-on case studies and the use of standard documentation, students will develop essential skills to effectively navigate global trade environments and contribute to trade operations in logistics and supply chain organizations.
