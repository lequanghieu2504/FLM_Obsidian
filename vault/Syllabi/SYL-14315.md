## Description
International Sea Freight Fowarding is a practice‑oriented course that equips student with the knowledge and skills to manage the full life cycle of sea freight operations. It focuses on how sea ports and terminals, freight forwarders, and shipping lines, and consignors work together to move cargo safely, quickly, and profitably in today’s global supply chains Throughout the course, real cases and examples will illustrate how sea freignt fowarding supports trade and logistics development in Vietnam, helping learners see clear links between theory and local industry practice. By the end of the module, students will be able to design and evaluate sea freight solutions, communicate effectively with international partners, and position yourself for careers with shipping lines, logistics service providers, and sea port operators.
