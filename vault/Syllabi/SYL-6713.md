## Description
Description: This course helps students to persuade employers, sell ideas to investors and pitch a client for new business. The course also introduces students to the tools and techniques they will need to convince people. For more details, it introduces and examines the STRONG method of pitching, which can be put to use immediately: Setting the Frame, Telling the Story, Revealing the Intrigue, Offering the Prize, Nailing the Hookpoint and Getting a Decision. Lecturer will use several teaching methods such as conducting group discussion and sharing field work experiences in the market on top of regular teaching. Students will have the chance to showcase their presentation and persuasion skills as well as their thinking process related to this field. Teaching methods: Direct Instruction, Inquiry-based Learning, Cooperative Learning, Case Study Analysis
