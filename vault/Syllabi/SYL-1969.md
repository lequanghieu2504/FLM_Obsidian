## Description
This course provides an introduction to visual communication design for students and provides them with the knowledge and skills to communicate their ideas and processes in a visual form more effectively. As a means of questioning and applying fundamental theories, concepts and techniques, students conduct research and produce practical projects. The course examines the basic elements of visual communication and how context shapes visual forms production and reception. Topics include: Visual Cues, Visual Theories, Visual Persuation, Visual Stereotypes and Visual Analysis. Case studies introduce major areas: Branding, Packaging, Advertisement, Animation, Web. Assessment includes periodic critique and review sessions that provide formative feedback on work in progress. '- This course will be organized in a studio - During class time, practice exercises will come right after each of theories. - Students are to research and hold mini seminars that focus on different majors areas in graphic design industry. - Students create art works after lectures and tutorials. These art works will be analyzed and evaluated by students under the control of lecturer. Finally, they will be graded by the lecturer. - Students collect all of their art works to make the portfolio.
