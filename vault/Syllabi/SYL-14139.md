## Description
The course "History of Design" explores the formation and evolution of design through various historical periods, from the Stone Age to contemporary movements. Situated at the intersection of art and science, the course provides students with a deep understanding of shifts in style, technology, and aesthetic perspectives over time, fostering an awareness of the cultural and social values that shape design. The course aims to equip students with foundational knowledge of the history and progression of design schools, while encouraging analytical and critical thinking within a historical context. Consequently, students can leverage these historical insights to identify, evaluate, and apply appropriate design elements to their future projects.
