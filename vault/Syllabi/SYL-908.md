## Description
The main objective of the course is to help students develop the academic language and academic skills especially Listening and Speaking skills. Besides, the course aims at providing students with strategies necessary to achieve proficiency in English. 1. Developing skills: 1.1 Listening skills: Prediction, Listening for signpost words, Classification, Understanding illustration: map, visual information, Note taking, Listening for paraphrase, etc. 1.2 Speaking skills: Giving factual information, Expressing opinions /attitudes /preferences / reasons/feelings, Describing, Comparing and contrasting, Speculation, etc. 2. Developing language: Increase academic vocabulary, including collocations, and sentence structures through the topics in academic context. 3. Dealing with tests/exams: provide students with different kinds of listening and speaking tasks simulating exams for practice.
