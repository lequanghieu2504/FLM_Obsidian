## Description
Objective: 'This project-based course equips students with language and soft skills including team-work and problem solving skills to be able to use English quite independently in common daily interactions and some limited academic situations Teaching method: lecture + acitivities + project works

## Learning Outcomes
| No. | CLO Name | CLO Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | CLO1 | Get the key message and some important details of speakers’ utterances in popular accents that contain some unfamiliar vocabulary without much difficulty. |
| 2 | CLO2 | Demonstrate understanding of the essential points and ability to respond when engaged in controlled conversations on topics such as narration of factual materials, description, or topics that involve familiar fields of knowledge with some lack of fluency. |
| 3 | CLO3 | Initiate, maintain and end face-to-face or telephone conversations on familiar topics containing some complex grammatical structures such as perfect tenses, passive voice, or some adverbial clauses |
| 4 | CLO4 | Identify main idea and key points to unfamiliar reading passages at the intermediate level |
| 5 | CLO5 | Demonstrate some basic critical thinking over others' opinions in written documents or speeches |
| 6 | CLO6 | Write simple, compound and some complex sentences with minor errors that do not affect understanding |
| 7 | CLO7 | Write a letter in the education domain and a narrative paragraph of approximately 100 words within 45 minutes |
| 8 | CLO8 | Actively and effectively apply group work and problem-solving skills in fulfilling projects |
