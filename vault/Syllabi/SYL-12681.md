## Description
This course equips students with comprehensive knowledge of electronic circuits, starting from the fundamentals of semiconductor materials and advancing to the design and analysis of amplifier, operational amplifier (Op-Amp), and signal conversion circuits. It covers essential topics including diodes, Bipolar Junction Transistors (BJTs), Field-Effect Transistors (FETs), analog-to-digital and digital-to-analog conversion, and the operation of timer circuits, with practical applications in circuit design. Through a combination of theoretical foundations and hands-on practice, students will develop skills to analyze circuit behavior, design efficient electronic systems, and utilize simulation software for modeling and testing. The course also emphasizes safety, professional ethics, and environmental considerations in electronic design.
