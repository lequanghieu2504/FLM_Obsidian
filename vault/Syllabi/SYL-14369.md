## Description
This course advances learners’ English proficiency for social, academic, and professional communication. The course develops mastery of complex grammar, vocabulary, and pronunciation while deepening awareness of cultural and global issues. Students interpret authentic materials, analyze information from various sources, and express opinions confidently in discussions or debates. Writing tasks emphasize clarity, organization, and accuracy. Learners apply technology and AI tools for efficient, ethical learning, demonstrate professionalism in collaboration, and use self-assessment strategies to achieve lifelong learning and global competence.
