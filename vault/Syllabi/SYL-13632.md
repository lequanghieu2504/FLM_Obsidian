## Description
This course provides core theories in multimedia production to prepare students for media production practice. It covers the entire media production process, from brainstorming ideas and content preparation to production execution. The scope of production varies from podcasts, webcasts, TVCs, promotional videos (promos), teasers, and trailers to movies and more. The course aims to help students practice with small-scale projects to become familiar with the techniques and the overall workflow.
