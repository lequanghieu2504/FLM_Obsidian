## Description
This course addresses several of the major ethical and policy issues that are changing the way engineers think about information and communication technologies This course will cover three broad categories Cybersafety, Cyberethics and Cybersecurity (C3) and how they are interrelated, providing the opportunity for vigorous discussion and exploration of the complex issues involved in C3 technology policy. Through a variety of case studies and group work, detailing IT issues, recent case law, position papers, original articles and resources, as well as scenarios for discussion, student will give consideration to responses that can be applied in their own organization.
