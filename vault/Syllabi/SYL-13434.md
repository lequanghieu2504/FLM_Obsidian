## Description
This course focuses on the complete process of character design for animated films, from creating rough representations based on research to executing full designs with color, turnaround sheets, facial expressions and action poses. Students will analyze characters, evaluate designs for improvement, and apply their knowledge to create character sets for 2D animations, while developing teamwork, self-study, and professional skills. The final outcome of the course is a product in digital format.
