## Description
This course is a continuation of basic electronic circuit course (EEA211). It mainly covers the important parts of analog electronic circuits as frequency response; operational amplifiers and their applications and power amplifier. Some advanced analog circuits with the emphasis on feedback and oscillator as well as linear-digital ICs are subsequently discussed. Finally, the topics of power supplies, other two-terminal devices and other semiconductor devices are described. Besides, some experiments are provided to help students to have a thorough grasp of the above discussion. Topics covered: BJT and JFET Frequency Response; Operational Amplifiers; Op-Amp Applications; Power Amplifiers; Linear-Digital ICs; Feedback and Oscillator Circuits; Power Supplies; Other Two-terminal Devices; pnpn and Other Devices.
