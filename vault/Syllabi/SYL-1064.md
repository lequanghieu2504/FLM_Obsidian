## Description
The couse will be divided into two main parts: - Part 1 is all about the advanced concept of MS Excel which consists of: In-Depth formating: students should acknowledge the advantages of MS Excel over MS Word in designing table-like documents. Charting: Data visualization are essential for Business Analyst and MS Excel is a perfect tool to support this aspect. While each type of chart in Excel has its unique usage, choosing the best chart is not an easy task. Functioning: the most used functions in Business will be discussed in the very last part (from the most simple ones like aggregation, sorting to advanced mode such as ranking or even conditional data searching) - Part 2 will be focused on programming in Excel using VBA environment: Creating new functions by their own in an advanced way for the topics covered in Part 1. Students do NOT need to have previous experiences in programming. However, since this part is a step by step guide so it is strongly recommended that paying attention and attending every lecture are the must.
