## Description
This course offers an essential guide to ethically navigating the complex legal terrain of media by addressing key issues such as the rights and liability of communicator/ media maker/ media user in classical and internet-based media; the intellectual property issues; and legal principles in press and publication. In this course, students will explore the risks and rights associated with media use and learn to manage intellectual property issues in this evolving landscape with an ethical approach. Upon completing this course, students are expected to achieve a comprehensive grasp of press and media law, journalism ethics, intellectual property rights, and communication responsibilities. They will learn to understand and apply ethical principles within various contexts, including press, communication, politics, and society. Students will also enhance their ability to critically analyze media laws and apply them to real-world situations. They will be skilled in constructing and communicating well-founded legal arguments with an ethical approach. Finally, the course will instill a professional, ethical, and responsible attitude towards media-related activities, preparing students to uphold the highest standards in their future media engagements.
