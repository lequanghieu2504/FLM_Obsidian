## Description
This course will aim to develop student’s knowledge and understanding of international economics and its application on international business. It will provide a deep contextualization of contemporary issues and debates within international business. The course prepares students the understanding of how the world economy is changing and how international business are deeply embedded in a complex web of relationships. The course will also explain globalization, trade theory and the way businesses can take advantage in contemporary world. The course’s ultimate goal is that students have a good understanding of theoretical, practical, and ethical issues associated with international economics and international business.
