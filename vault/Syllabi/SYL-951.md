## Description
This course will prepare you for further study in higher level topics of 3D animation. In this course, you will learn all the tools to create your own animation and execute the entire visual production.

## Learning Outcomes
| No. | CLO Name | CLO Details |
| --- | --- | --- |

| 2 | CLO2 | In depth study In modeling construction and anatomy study |

| 4 | CLO4 | Understand the production process of 3D animation/modeling |
| 5 | CLO5 | During this course, students are required to complete an animated film, duration about 2 minutes |

| 22 | The Twelve Basic Priciples of Animation (part 1) The purpose of animation is to serve the story e to capture images of life, and in the process communicate to others, even if it just means giving them a thrill ride. Animators at Walt Disney Studio wanted to create a kind of animation that felt real and let the audience respond to the character and story. As they observed and practiced what was at the time a new art, they developed a set of guidelines for animating. Two of these pioneers, Frank Thomas and Ollie Johnston, set them out in 1981 in their book The Illusion of Life: Disney Animation. These guidelines have become standard for the animation industry.
