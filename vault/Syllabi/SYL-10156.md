## Description
This course aims at: - Introducing advanced and complex grammar concepts to students - Instructing students to blend verb tenses effectively and how to punctuate sentences correctly - Helping students improve their academic English *Assessment scheme: 1) Ongoing assessment: Student gets 0.25 bonus points for each course completed on time. The total bonus point is not greater than 1. 2) Theoretical Exam (TE) 3) Final Result (FR) =min (10, TE+ Bonus) 4) Completion Criteria: TE>=4 and FR>=5

## Learning Outcomes
| No. | CLO Name | CLO Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | CLO1 | Review all verb tenses; and practice blending and mixing tenses in English LO1.1 Review verb tenses in English LO1.2 Fill in any gaps of understanding of verb tenses LO1.3 Blend and mix tenses in producing language |
| 2 | CLO2 | Develop complex sentences using conjunctions and connective devices LO2.1 Use conjunctions in sentences LO2.2 Use connectives in sentences LO2.3 Use subordinating conjunctions and make adverb clauses LO2.4 Reduce adverb clauses to make complex sentences |
| 3 | CLO3 | Practice using noun clauses and conditionals in advanced speaking and writing LO3.1 Identify and apply different types of noun clauses in writing and speaking LO3.2 Practice conditionals and mix them in English |
| 4 | CLO4 | Practice acquired advanced grammar points in writing LO4.1 Create a graphic organizer to help remember grammar points LO4.2 Practice use various grammar points in writing |
