## Description
This course introduces students to foundational writing skills for media across various platforms. Through a structured, step-by-step approach, students will learn to effectively produce content in different media formats. Emphasis will be placed on developing the writing competencies necessary for success in professional media environments. Additionally, the course will address the core principles of credibility in media writing, alongside legal and ethical considerations in both print and online journalism. Where appropriate, selected media writing formats will also be introduced in Vietnamese to help students understand format adaptation in local professional contexts. Course Objectives: Upon successful completion of this course, students will be able to: - Understand and apply key concepts in media-related business administration, management, and communication within cultural, legal, societal, and psychological contexts. - Design and implement effective communication projects tailored to audience needs, utilizing appropriate media formats and technologies. - Create media content suitable for multiple platforms with persuasive messaging based on research, relevant knowledge, and creative thinking. - Demonstrate proficiency in using English for learning and professional communication, while recognizing and applying selected Vietnamese media writing formats where appropriate. Academic Integrity and Conduct: - All forms of academic misconduct, including copyright infringement, plagiarism, and fraud during coursework or examinations, are strictly prohibited. - Any assignment found to have over 20% similarity (as checked by Turnitin) may be considered an intentional act of plagiarism and subject to disciplinary measures. Use of AI Tools: AI tools can serve as valuable educational aids when used appropriately. Students are expected to adhere to the following guidelines: A. Acceptable Uses of AI Tools: 1. Brainstorming and idea generation 2. Grammar and proofreading support 3. Clarifying complex concepts 4. Creating practice exercises 5. Organizing research notes 6. Data visualization 7. Image or video creation (with proper referencing) All AI-generated content must be appropriately cited. Unacknowledged use will be treated as academic dishonesty and reviewed on a case-by-case basis. B. Inappropriate Uses of AI Tools: 1. Completing assignments or exams that assess individual understanding 2. Generating essays or reports in lieu of student work 3. Circumventing the development of independent research and analytical skills
