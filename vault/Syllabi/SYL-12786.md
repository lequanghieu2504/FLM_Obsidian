## Description
"-Objective: The course is designed to provide students with theoretical knowledge and practical scenarios related to public service interpreting in different settings including health care, media, police, military services, business, job training programs.. -Description: The course is composed of theoretical lectures familiarizing students with different interpreting settings of public services. Based on authentic audio and video language materials related to health care, media, police, military services... scenarios are created for learners to take turn to accomplish their roles as interpreters. -Teaching method: Constructive Teaching Method is highly recommended. During the course, lectures may take place in different forms including traditional classroom, conference-styled classrooms, or outdoor activities.
