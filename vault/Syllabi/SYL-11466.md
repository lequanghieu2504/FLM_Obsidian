## Description
Learn how to keep your audience engaged by focusing on the content and delivery of public address in this course. It is the responsibility of the tourism-related lecturer to provide each student with a variety of themes and requirements for their presentations. Most importantly, the training will help students gain the confidence and skills necessary to deal with a wide range of circumstances and customer files in the tourism sector (narrative about scenic spots, presentation of highlights of local culture, introduction and typical tourist attractions of the country, .. are topics that should be covered in the course)
