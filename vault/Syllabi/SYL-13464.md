## Description
Description: This course builds the skills to turn ideas into industry-standard scripts for short films and TVCs. Students analyze and apply narrative structure and character arcs; produce a 3–5 minute short-film script and a 30–45 second TVC script/storyboard; and collaborate through critique and pitching. AI tools may support ideation and drafting, but usage must be documented and ethical, with originality and authorship maintained.
