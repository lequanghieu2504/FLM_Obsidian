## Description
This course provides essential knowledge and practical skills to apply Artificial Intelligence in optimizing communication strategies in data-rich environments. Students will learn to collect, organize, and analyze structured and unstructured data using generative AI tools and prompt engineering techniques. The course emphasizes extracting actionable insights, visualizing data, and developing custom GPT-based assistants to support analytical and communication tasks. Ethical considerations and best practices in AI-assisted analysis are also addressed, preparing students to implement data-driven, AI-enhanced strategies across diverse communication contexts.
