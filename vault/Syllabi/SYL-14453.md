## Description
This course equips students with knowledge about car structures, including knowledge related to the structure and operating principles of cars. This knowledge will help students understand cars and be helpful in their future work in programming car systems. The module content also introduces software applications in the automotive industry. In addition, the module also equips students with career orientation, soft skills as well as professional ethical foundation, customer care and initial experiences with cars.
