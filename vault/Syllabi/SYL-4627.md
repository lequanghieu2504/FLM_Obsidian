# SYL-4627

Degree Level: Bachelor
Time Allocation: 15 hours (1 credit): - 10 online sessions (1 session = 90 minutes) - 1 final exam includes Theoretical Exam (30 minutes)

Active: True

Decision: 1293/QĐ-ĐHFPT dated 12/02/2020

## Description
This course help students to explore blockchain platforms including, 2 permissioned blockchain platforms: Linux Foundation’s Hyperledger Fabric và Microsoft Azure’s Blockchain as a Service; 2 Dapp platforms: Augur and Grid+ ; Important Challenges and solutions to develop blockchain platform; other decentralized solutions with Interplanetary File System (IPFS) và Hashgraph; and especially explore 2 blockchain platforms developed by Vietnam: Akachain, Tomochain
