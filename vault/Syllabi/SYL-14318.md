## Description
The Digital Video Editing course aims to equip students with fundamental knowledge of the digital video industry. Students will learn to master the entire video production process, along with essential techniques such as scripting, filming, editing, post-production, and audio processing. The course introduces key stages of video production, including script development, production planning, editing, sound design, post-production, and the publishing of final products. Students will work with software such as Adobe Premiere, Adobe Audition, Adobe After Effects, and other supporting tools. The course also introduces several Artificial Intelligence (AI) tools used in the video production process, enabling students to explore and apply these advanced technologies to enhance the efficiency and quality of their media products.
