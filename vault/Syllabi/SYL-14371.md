## Description
This course is an advanced English course that refines learners’ ability to communicate with precision, fluency, and cultural sophistication in academic and professional settings. Students synthesize ideas from diverse authentic sources, analyze global and ethical issues, and evaluate meaning, bias, and rhetorical stance in complex spoken and written texts. Emphasis is placed on persuasive writing, academic discussion, and critical thinking. Learners apply technology and AI tools to enhance research and collaboration while demonstrating leadership, professionalism, and lifelong learning for global engagement.
