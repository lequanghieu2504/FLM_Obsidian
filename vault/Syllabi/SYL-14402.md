## Description
Goods and Insurance is a course that introduces fundamental knowledge about goods in commercial, transportation, and logistics activities, while also highlighting the role of insurance in protecting goods against risks arising during storage, handling, and transportation. The course focuses on topics such as the characteristics and classification of goods, requirements for packaging, labeling, and storage, common risks associated with goods, basic principles of insurance, cargo insurance conditions, and the claims process in case of loss or damage. Through this course, learners can understand the relationship between the nature of different types of goods and their corresponding risk levels, thereby enabling them to select appropriate insurance methods to minimize losses, support risk management, and enhance efficiency in logistics and international trade operations.

## Learning Outcomes
| No. | CLO Name | CLO Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | CLO1 | Explain the fundamental concepts of goods and insurance in commercial, transport, and logistics activities, including the characteristics, classification, packaging, marking, storage, and risk exposure of goods. |
| 2 | CLO2 | Identify and classify different types of goods, especially dangerous goods, based on their characteristics, hazardous properties, and relevant transport requirements in logistics operations. |
| 3 | CLO3 | Apply international regulations and standard procedures related to the transport of goods, including the classification, packing, marking, labelling, and documentation requirements for dangerous goods shipments. |
| 4 | CLO4 | Analyze the roles, responsibilities, and training requirements of the parties involved in the transport and transit chain in order to ensure compliance and safety in goods handling and transportation. |
| 5 | CLO5 | Apply the principal concepts of cargo insurance, including insurable interest, indemnity, subrogation, marine risks, and types of losses, to assess risk exposure and determine suitable insurance protection for different categories of goods. |
| 6 | CLO6 | Evaluate cargo loss or damage situations and perform appropriate actions, including selecting suitable insurance cover under Institute Cargo Clauses (A), (B), and (C), preparing transport documentation, and handling insurance claims effectively. |
