## Description
This course instructs students how to apply translation techniques in dealing with interlingual translation of different types of texts including administrative forms and documents (degrees, certificate, contracts..); business forms and documents (business transaction agreement, financial statements); technical news and documents; socio-cultural news and documents. After taking this course, students should be able to: - Demonstrate a clear understanding of different types and formats of texts - Demonstrate a clear understanding terms used the administrative documents, technical, economic, scientific, socio-cultural news and documents... - Translate the mentioned kinds of news and documents from Vietnamese into English and vice-versa. - Use Gen-AI tools to enhance translation quality and facilitate translation processes
