## Description
The course is designed to develop and sharpen learners' academic and professional writing skills and strategies in English. Each unit guides the student through the structured writing process with plenty of full colour illustrations, examples, and exercises to engage the student and build confidence. This course aims at providing students understanding and skills from writing a paragragh to developing a full essay.
