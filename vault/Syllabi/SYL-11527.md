## Description
This course is a follow-up to FIN202 and IBF301, in which students touch on fundamental concepts of finance in general and corporate finance in particular. After the theoretical foundation established in FIN202 and IBF301, students now have chance to acquire indepth knowledge about Derivatives valuation and investment strategies. Topics include: Futures and Forwards Valuation and Strategies, Option Valuation and Strategies, Swaps, Financial Risk Management.

## Learning Outcomes
| No. | CLO Name | CLO Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | CLO1 | Gain the essential knowledge and expertise in managing financial risks within the context of international investments, along with an understanding of the tools available for mitigating such risks. |
| 2 | CLO2 | Effectively utilize comprehension of derivatives to tackle issues related to financial risks, specifically those encountered in international investments. This includes addressing challenges such as foreign exchange risk, interest rate risk, credit risk, and portfolio risks. |
| 3 | CLO3 | Make well-informed assessments regarding the utilization of derivative instruments, taking into account the specific requirements and considerations associated with international investments. |
| 4 | CLO4 | Recognize and evaluate the influence of significant advancements in the literature pertaining to derivatives, particularly in relation to their impact on the field of international investments. |
| 5 | CLO5 | Recognize and practice a commitment to professional ethical standard. |
| 6 | CLO6 | Display teamwork and conduct task both independently and collaboratively |
