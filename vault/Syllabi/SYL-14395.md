## Description
The course gives students a working understanding of how modern generative AI models work and how to use them well. The course covers foundations and intuition of large language models, diffusion and multimodal models; prompt engineering and evaluation; retrieval-augmented generation (RAG); building applications with model APIs; agents and tool use; customization (prompting, RAG, fine-tuning); failure modes and evaluation; and ethics, safety, and governance. Mathematical internals are deferred to the Deep Learning course; this course stays at the level of intuition while building real, hands-on skill.

| 2 | CLO2 | Apply prompt engineering techniques to obtain useful, reliable outputs from generative models. |
| 3 | CLO3 | Build a small generative AI application end to end, retrieval-augmented generation, structured output validation, basic agent/tool use, and an appropriate customization strategy (prompting, RAG, or fine-tuning). |
| 4 | CLO4 | Evaluate generative AI outputs and systems using rubrics, test sets, model-as-judge methods, and analysis of failure modes. |
| 5 | CLO5 | Analyze the ethical, social, legal, and governance implications of generative AI and apply responsible-use practices. |
