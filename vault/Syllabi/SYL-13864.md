## Description
This course will cover the history, theory and practice of letterforms and typography as they apply to graphics, advertising and other areas of design and visual communication. Projects cover typography history, principles of typography, letter structure, typeface selection, fundamentals of computer typesetting.

## Learning Outcomes
| No. | CLO Name | CLO Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | CLO1 | Classify typographic styles by period, noting key traits and contexts. |
| 2 | CLO2 | Explain typeface anatomy and how proportions, stroke dynamics, and metrics/spacing affect legibility and readability. |
| 3 | CLO3 | Evaluate typographic approaches for a given goal and their impact on meaning and hierarchy. |
| 4 | CLO4 | Design brief-fit typographic solutions with appropriate pairing, hierarchy/grid/rhythm, and correct specs. |
| 5 | CLO5 | Use technology tools and AI tools effectively across the typography workflow with responsible disclosure and verification. |
