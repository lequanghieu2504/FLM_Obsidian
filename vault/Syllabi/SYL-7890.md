# SYL-7890

Subject: [[OJK202]]
Name: Đào tạo trong môi trường thực tế
Credits: 10
Degree Level: Bachelor
Time Allocation: 14 weeks ~ 560 hours
Prerequisite: Complete 90% of total credits from semester 1 to semester 5 (excluding National Defense and Physical Education Subject)
Active: True
Approved: True
Decision: 796/QĐ-ĐHFPT dated 07/21/2021

## Description
Students are sent to enterprises in fields related to their specialty, experiencing a real work process under a plan previously agreed by FPT University and the enterprise, students will have the opportunity to apply the knowledge and skills that they have gained, capture more learnings, set goals and make the choice of their academic concentration at the end of the internship.

## Learning Outcomes
| No. | CLO Name | CLO Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | CLO1 | Knowledge and specialized skills: Apply knowledge and skills learned to successfully complete assigned tasks. |
| 2 | CLO2 | Soft skills: Ability to grasp and handle situations at work, understand and integrate with the culture of the receiving enterprise. |
| 3 | CLO3 | Attitude to work: Comply with discipline, work in a serious and strict manner, have a spirit of teamwork… |
