## Description
This course is designed for speakers at all levels, especially those who feel nervous about public speaking. Through gradual, hands-on practice, learners build confidence and master key speaking skills before delivering full speeches. Students will learn the basics of public speaking and how to communicate effectively in different contexts: explaining complex ideas clearly with engaging slides, persuading audiences with strong arguments and impactful language, and inspiring listeners through ceremonial and motivational speeches. In the applied learning project, learners design, practice, record, and receive feedback on speeches they can use in real-life situations. By the end of the course, they will be able to create compelling presentations and develop a portfolio of polished speeches.
