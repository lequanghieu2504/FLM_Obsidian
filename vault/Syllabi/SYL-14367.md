## Description
This course develops learners’ ability to communicate effectively in everyday and social situations. It expands vocabulary, grammar, and pronunciation for describing people, places, daily routines, and experiences. Students practice listening for key information and speaker intentions, engage in short conversations, and express ideas using connected sentences with clear pronunciation. Reading and writing tasks focus on understanding and producing short, organized texts related to daily life and culture. The course also integrates information technology and AI tools to support learning, promotes teamwork, professionalism, and ethical communication, and encourages self-assessment to build autonomy and lifelong learning skills for global communication.

## Learning Outcomes
| No. | CLO Name | CLO Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | CLO1 | Recognize and explain English vocabulary, grammar structures, and pronunciation patterns used in social and everyday interactions |
| 2 | CLO2 | Apply common expressions and cultural norms appropriately in routine social, study, and work-related situations. |
| 3 | CLO3 | Identify key information, details, and speaker intentions in short dialogues, travel announcements, service interactions, and conversations about daily life topics. |
| 4 | CLO4 | Describe key information, engage in short spoken interactions and discussions on familiar topics using connected sentences, appropriate intonation, and clear pronunciation. |
| 5 | CLO5 | Interpret, analyze, and summarize information from short articles, schedules, and dialogues on daily life and cultural topics, using reading strategies to understand meaning and context. |
| 6 | CLO6 | Write short, connected sentences or a short paragraph about familiar matters, and uses simple connectors, basic tenses, and topic-related vocabulary with reasonable accuracy. |
| 7 | CLO7 | Apply information technology and artificial intelligence (AI) tools to enhance English learning efficiently, ethically, and responsibly. |
| 8 | CLO8 | Work independently and collaboratively with responsibility, demonstrating respect, professionalism, and ethical behavior in communication and academic activities. |
| 9 | CLO9 | Develop a personal learning plan using self-assessment and technology to monitor progress, reflect on performance, and improve English proficiency for lifelong learning and global communication. |
