## Description
This course gives student basic concepts and knowledge related to Interaction Design and UX aspect of Interaction Design. It addresses the principles of usable design such as consistent, aesthetic, system status feedback and so on. In this course, student will obtain knowledge of understanding users, understanding the interactions, designing for collaboration and communication, understanding how interface affect users and etc. After this course, the student have ability to collect the needs and the requirement for a product, to follow a standard process to design the product from collected requirements, to develop prototypes to demonstration his/her design ideas, to construct the design product and then performs a simple test designs such as usability testing to verify successes or uncover flaws of the design, and to improve the design products afterward.

## Learning Outcomes
| No. | CLO Name | CLO Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | CLO1 | Discuss about definition of Interaction Design, User Experience design goal and basic concepts and general principles related to those activities. |
| 2 | CLO2 | Understand interactions and how to conceptualize it by identifying the problem spaces, building up conceptual models, selecting interface metaphors, and promoting interaction paradigms |
| 3 | CLO3 | Understand about users, applying cognition knowledge into UX design, conceptual frameworks for cognition such as mental models, information processing and external cognition. |
| 4 | CLO4 | Understand social mechanisms used in collaboration and communication such as Conversional, Coordination and Awareness and the collaborative technologies has been designed to support those mechanisms. |
| 5 | CLO5 | Understand the application of anthropomorphism to interaction design to improve User Experience. |
| 6 | CLO6 | Understand about Interaction and User Experience design lifecycles, how to collect data from users, how to design and demonstrate by prototyping and the importance of evaluation in the design process. |
| 7 | CLO7 | Identify, collect and manage the needs of users and the requirements. Planning and Prepare for UX research and design works |
| 8 | CLO8 | Decide which interface metaphors, interaction paradigms, interaction mechanism and anthropomorphism should be applied or not, and determine which will be resulted into the product. |
| 9 | CLO9 | Design an interaction products for user experience by prototyping. |
| 10 | CLO10 | Perform usability testing and evaluations techniques to point out improvement aspects of a design product and then improve the design. |
