## Description
This course provides students with a fundamental understanding of measurement and control engineering. It covers the principles, performance characteristics, and elements of measurement systems, including sensors, transducers, and signal processing. Students will learn to model and analyze system dynamics, transfer functions, and both time-domain and frequency-domain responses. The course also introduces basic control concepts such as feedback, stability, compensation, and PID control, enabling students to design and assess simple control systems. Through practical exercises and simulations, students will develop the skills to perform measurements, collect and interpret data, and evaluate the behavior of measurement and control systems.
