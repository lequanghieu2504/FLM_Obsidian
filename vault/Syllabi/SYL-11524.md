## Description
Main objectives: - Forming research orientation in the project development process: specifically in the culture and commerce environment - Applying the essential skills for the final project - Getting students to apply the knowledge they have learned and researched in the field of advertising communication: brand identity, packaging, information graphics... to come up with products that are practical and highly applicable. - Students must work in a group. The number of members for each group, decided by the Head Academic of campus, could be variable from 2 to 3 students The project is done in 4 stages with feedback and discussion at each stage. Students are expected to discuss, organise, present and critique their work with a high level of professionalism, and implement research strategies that contribute to the overall finished project 1) Progress on Project (04 reports): 45% - Report 1: 15% - Report 2: 10% - Report 3: 10% - Report 4: 10% Mark of each report must be >= 4/10; 2) Final Product and Caspton Document (by instructor): 5%; Final Product and Caspton Document (by instructor) >=4 3) Project Defense - Final product and Capstone Project Document (by council):20%; - Presentation (Oral Project Defense): 30% Project Defense >=5 4) Final results:100%; Final Result >=5/10 Final Product and Caspton Document must be submitted before deadline
