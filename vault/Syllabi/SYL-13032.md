## Description
This course is derived from the Coursera Specialization "HRIC Human Resource Associate Professional Certificate." Human Resource (HR) professionals are crucial to an organization's success, playing an essential role in recruitment and management of right candidates and talents, policy development, and fostering a positive work environment. Pursuing a career in HR enables individuals to significantly contribute to an organization's overall achievements. This program is meticulously designed to prepare aspiring HR professionals. It encompasses five modules, offering a diverse array of engaging videos, interactive activities, assessments, and peer-reviewed projects aimed at enhancing professional expertise and providing a comprehensive understanding of Human Resource Management (HRM) fundamentals. Upon completion of this program, students will understand how to: - Recruit, select, and onboard new employees effectively. - Develop and implement employee policies and procedures that align with organizational objectives. - Foster a positive work culture and enhance employee engagement. - Understand and navigate legal and ethical considerations in HR. Graduates of this program will receive a certificate from Coursera and gain the requisite knowledge and skills to excel in the HR field. Additionally, they will be prepared to undertake the HRCI Associate Professional in Human Resources (aPHR) certification exam, distinguishing themselves as qualified HR professionals.
