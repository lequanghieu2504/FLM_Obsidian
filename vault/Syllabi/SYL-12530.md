## Description
The UI/UX Design Specialization brings a design-centric approach to user interface and user experience design, and offers practical, skill-based instruction centered around a visual communications perspective, rather than on one focused on marketing or programming alone. In this sequence of four courses, students will summarize and demonstrate all stages of the UI/UX development process, from user research to defining a project’s strategy, scope, and information architecture, to developing sitemaps and wireframes. Students’ll learn current best practices and conventions in UX design and apply them to create effective and compelling screen-based experiences for websites or apps. Final Exam is included of Final Theory Exam (TE) & Final Practical Exam (PE): 100% Students who complete all MOOCs before the course deadline will receive 1 bonus point; otherwise, no bonus point will be awarded. Condition to pass: TE>=4 and PE>=4 and FR = (TE+PE)/2 + bonus >=5
