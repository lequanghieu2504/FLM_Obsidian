## Description
This course introduces multimedia elements, theories, and hardware/software components of multimedia including image, graphics, sound, and video components. In related with the multimedia elements, students learn the fundamental of multimedia processing, multimedia design, including principles of graphic design;introduction to authoring; on-screen layout; and introduction to authoring tools. This class will also include a brief introduction to authoring software. By teaching not only fundamental technologies but also emerging technologies, the course provides the most essential skills in handling multimedia tools and designing multimedia content in a evelopment environment.
