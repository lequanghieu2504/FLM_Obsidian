## Description
This course aims to lay the groundwork for addressing complex problems, engaging stakeholders, and offering tools for collecting and analyzing data to tackle community transformation challenges in a comprehensive way. Learners will have the chance to draft a proposal based on systems thinking principles, describe and visualize the system and its components relevant to a specific project focus, apply systems thinking and project management concepts, and collaborate with stakeholders to assess and identify community needs.

## Learning Outcomes
| No. | CLO Name | CLO Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | CLO1 | Apply systems thinking to identify and analyze complex community challenges, integrating sustainability into project planning. |
| 2 | CLO2 | Master data collection and analysis techniques, to inform evidence-based decision-making for community transformation initiatives. |
| 3 | CLO3 | Develop comprehensive project proposals using systems mapping and stakeholder analysis to address identified needs effectively. |
| 4 | CLO4 | Evaluate community transformation strategies through the lens of sustainability leadership, considering economic, social, and environmental impacts to drive long-term value creation. |
| 5 | CLO5 | Collaborate effectively in cross-functional teams to achieve shared transformation goals. |
