## Description
Wharton’s Fintech Specialization introduces the core concepts of financial technology and its real-world applications. Through case studies, you’ll explore topics like cryptocurrency, portfolio optimization, digital payments, robo-advising, and blockchain. By the end, you’ll be equipped to make informed decisions about using fintech solutions for personal or business advantage.

## Learning Outcomes
| No. | CLO Name | CLO Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | CLO1 | Understand FinTech Fundamentals: Grasp the core concepts, technologies, and evolution of FinTech, and their transformative impact on financial services. |
| 2 | CLO2 | Analyze Applications of Technology in Finance: Evaluate the integration of emerging technologies like AI, blockchain, and data analytics into payments, lending, investing, and insurance. |
| 3 | CLO3 | Examine Market Dynamics and Consumer Trends: Identify and interpret market trends, consumer behavior, and the factors driving the adoption of FinTech innovations. |
| 4 | CLO4 | Navigate Regulatory and Ethical Challenges: Understand the regulatory frameworks and ethical considerations, including data privacy and cybersecurity, in the FinTech ecosystem. |
| 5 | CLO5 | Critically Assess Risks and Opportunities: Analyze risks such as cybersecurity threats and operational challenges while identifying opportunities for growth and innovation in financial technology. |
| 6 | CLO6 | Apply Strategic Thinking to Financial Innovation: Develop the skills to apply FinTech solutions to real-world problems, enhancing efficiency and value in financial operations. |
| 7 | CLO7 | Present the understanding of the subject as well as skills through individual work and group work. |
