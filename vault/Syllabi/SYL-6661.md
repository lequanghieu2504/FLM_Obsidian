## Description
This course is aimed to give you the basics you need to start working with IoT. Students are required to carry out projects in laboratories as assigned by teachers (mentors), through sample tutorials, students can do IoT applications in practice, training students the basic skills of practicing technical professions in the field of control through the Internet to connect things. Group size is limited to 1-2 students, appointed by the faculty (mentor)
