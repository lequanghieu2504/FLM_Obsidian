## Description
This course prepares English major students for successful teaching experience with theory and practice in classroom management through authentic teaching, provides students with opportunities to apply AI and other digital tools to optimize teaching and learning processes. Emphasis is placed on developing efficiency, accuracy, and personalized learning, as well as cultivating future-oriented pedagogical competencies. Teaching method: lectures + teaching performance + reflection

## Learning Outcomes
| No. | CLO Name | CLO Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | CLO1 | Construct SMART ESL/EFL lesson outcomes to meet learners' needs |
| 2 | CLO2 | Integrate appropriate instructional strategies to meet an ESL/EFL lesson's outcomes |
| 3 | CLO3 | Design and generate pedagogically sound ESL/EFL lesson ideas through the effective use of AI and digital learning tools, ensuring alignment with specified lesson outcomes and curriculum standards. |
| 4 | CLO4 | Implement AI-supported instructional strategies that respond to learners’ linguistic backgrounds, individual needs, and affective factors such as motivation and language anxiety. |
| 5 | CLO5 | Critically evaluate the effectiveness of ESL/EFL lesson demonstrations by utilizing AI-assisted observation and reflection tools to identify pedagogical strengths and areas for enhancement. |
| 6 | CLO6 | Justify, through evidence-based reasoning, the influence of teacher behaviour and AI-supported classroom interactions on learners’ motivation and engagement. |
| 7 | CLO7 | Apply AI-driven assessment and feedback systems to monitor, analyse, and improve learner performance and language development in ESL/EFL contexts. |
| 8 | CLO8 | Demonstrate ethical awareness and professional responsibility in the use of AI technologies for language teaching, ensuring fairness, data privacy, and inclusivity in learning environments. |
