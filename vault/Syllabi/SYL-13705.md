## Description
This course is derived from the Coursera Specialization "Advanced Digital Transformation Specialization." This specialization is composed of three courses. Each offers a different perspectives on digital technology in the strategic context. Whether you’re an IT professional looking to move up or a seasoned executive hoping to keep up, this specialization will help you drive change that matters. Let’s dive into the specifics 1. Leading with a Digital Strategy (Course 1) Understand digital capabilities and their strategic impact Explore lenses for conceptualizing digital transformation Learn about key concepts underlying successful digital initiatives Discover how scientific thinking plays a crucial role in mastering digital transformation 2. How to Transform Digitally? (Course 2) Identify notable strategies and business models for successful digital transformations, in the modern age characterized by advanced and artificially intelligent technologies Transform digital elements (Digital Architecture, Work, Governance, Business Models) effectively. Analyze challenges and learn the DaWoGoMo model for organizational transformation Explore the operational purpose driving digital change 3. Transforming Life and Organization in the Digital Age (Course 3) Understand major technological trends driving digital transformation of our lives Learn the evolutionary and natural purposes driving digital transformation. Champion initiatives for your life and growth by mastering the existential purpose Identify business and life opportunities arising from digital transformation
