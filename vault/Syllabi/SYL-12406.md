# SYL-12406

## Description
This course is the continuation of SAP311. In this course, students will learn how to operate modules WM (Ware House Management), FI (Finance Accouting), and CO (Controlling).Theory, case study and practical sessions are arranged alternately, helping students understand the operation of these modules.

## Learning Outcomes
| No. | CLO Name | CLO Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | CLO1 | Describe the process of Ware House Management (WM), the central organizational structures of the FI and CO module |
| 2 | CLO2 | Perform simple operations on WM, FI, CO modules |
| 3 | CLO3 | Analyze business case studies and apply SAP system to handle situations |
| 4 | CLO4 | Evaluate all the tasks performed to solve the case studies |
| 5 | CLO5 | Develop professional working style, good teamwork skills and compliance with the production process. |
