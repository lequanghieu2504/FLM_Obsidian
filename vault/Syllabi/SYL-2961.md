## Description
Housekeeping management examines the role of the supervisory function in the housekeeping department, provides a thorough overview of maintaining a quality staff, planning and organizing, the technical details of cleaning a room, managing the laundary, and control of supplies and equipment.

## Learning Outcomes
| No. | CLO Name | CLO Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | CLO1 | To describe the role of the housekeeping department in hotel operations, and explain the importance of effective communication between housekeeping department and other departments |
| 2 | CLO2 | To list and describe criteria for selection of materials and equipment for the hotel, for example, walls, floorings, beddings, linen, carpets, etc. |
| 3 | CLO3 | To discuss the proper procedure of cleaning and inspecting guest rooms, public areas and others, as well as able to demonstrate a working knowledge of the procedures. |
| 4 | CLO4 | To explain usage of various tools used to plan and organize the housekeeping department – staffing guide, area inventory lists, frequency schedules, performance standards and productivity standards. |
| 5 | CLO5 | To utilize various techniques to manage inventories effectively |
| 6 | CLO6 | To utilize various methods to control expenses in the housekeeping department |
