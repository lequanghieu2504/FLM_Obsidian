## Description
The course provides a framework for interpretation, analysis and evaluation of financial statements from viewpoints of creditors, owners, investment firms and others. Thereby, the users of accounting information understand business strengths or weakness, the impact of general business and specific industry situations, behavior of financial markets, credit or lending criteria, and equity investment standards on present and future company financial conditions. After completing the course, the student shall be able to: - Be familiar with the organization and disclosure of reported information - Quantitatively and qualitatively evaluate core accounting issues that are particularly relevant in the context of financial statement analysis. - Have a framework for forecasting future earnings and financial performance. - Evaluate the business financial performance by applying quantitative and qualitative techniques. - Use financial statement information in a modeling environment to identify and recommend solutions to a variety of financial challenges, and to recommend investment decisions.
