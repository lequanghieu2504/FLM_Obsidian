## Description
This course will describe five primary stages in multimedia project, determine the scope of a multimedia project. They may include phrases, tasks, cost, timeline, elements of a multimedia project proposal. In addition, this course gives students opportunities to practice in a real-life project. Lecturer will use several teaching methods such as conducting group discussion and sharing field work experiences in the market on top of regular teaching. Students will have the chance to present their work as well as their thinking process related to this firm..
