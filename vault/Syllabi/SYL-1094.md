## Description
This course is a model-based introduction to RE, providing the conceptual backgroud and terminology on RE, addressing a variety of techniques for - Analysis and Requirements Elicitation; - Requirements Evaluation; - Requirements Specification and Documentation; - Requirements Quality Assurance and demonstrating the Goal-Orientated Aproach in Requirements Engineering through - Modeling System Objectives with Goal Diagrams - Modeling Conceptual objects with Class diagrams - Moddeling System Agents and Responsibilities - Moddeling System Operations. During the course students are required to practice the RE techniques in class exercises and Assignments
