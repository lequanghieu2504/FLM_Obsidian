## Description
This course provides an overview of how information systems support business operations, decision-making, and digital transformation in modern organizations. Students will explore core topics such as business processes, enterprise systems, databases, networks, cybersecurity, e-commerce, knowledge management and artificial intelligence. The course emphasizes how organizations use information systems strategically to achieve operational excellence, improve decision-making, and gain competitive advantage, so that learners know how information systems are transforming global business, and why they are so essential for running and managing a business today. Through real-world case studies and practical activities, students will analyze the structure of digital firms, model business processes, and evaluate system solutions. Key technologies such as cloud computing, data analytics, and business intelligence are introduced. Ethical and social issues related to data and systems use are also discussed. The course bridges business needs with IT capabilities, equipping students with foundational knowledge to work at the intersection of business and technology.
