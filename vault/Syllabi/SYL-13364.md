## Description
'-Objective: By the end of the course, students will learn the basic knowledge of photography include: + Foundational history of photography. + Visual language and aesthetics of photography. At the end of the course, students will be able to: + Complete an final album including story, style, composition, lighting, concept and aesthetics values + Have many practical experiences. - Description: In this course, students will learn about: Foundational history of photography; Classification of photography. Greatest photographers; Structure of camera; Camera operation; Foundational composition and visual language of photography; Handling post-production; Aesthetic in photography.

## Learning Outcomes
| No. | CLO Name | CLO Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | CLO1 | Understand the history and theory of photography, the relationship between photography and visual disciplines, and its influence on culture. |
| 2 | CLO2 | Understand both advanced digital and analog camera operations with the understanding of formats, exposure in photography, depth of field, shutter speed, and illustrate proper placement of plane of focus. |
| 3 | CLO3 | Illustration of advanced visual literacy skills by utilizing sophisticated compositional properties and visual qualities of a photographic image |
| 4 | CLO4 | Demonstrate the ability to recognize and control both natural and studio lighting with emphasis on the quality, quantity, direction of light and the resulting effect of light on the photographic image, and ability to determine what type of studio lighting equipment is necessary to properly light various types of objects, people, spaces, and locations. |
| 5 | CLO5 | Manipulate, manage, archive digital images and control the transition of image color, tone and value from screen to printed image. |
| 6 | CLO6 | Organize, edit, and evaluate a professional quality portfolio of photographic work that demonstrates creative problem solving skill and effective communication. |
| 7 | CLO7 | Having a positive attitude and a professional working style |
