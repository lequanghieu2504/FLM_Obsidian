## Description
The course aims at helping students use the most common and powerful images editing tool – Photoshop. Photoshop is considered to be a magician of images, provides users with different images editing tools, from very simple like crop, resize to more complicated function such as images effects. The subject focuses on fundamental images editing skills for website, advertising and brand identity design.
