## Description
- This practical module emphasizes hands-on experience with embedded systems by assembling, wiring, and interfacing hardware components with microcontroller boards. Students will practice programming and operating electrical, electronic, and microcontroller devices, reinforcing the theoretical knowledge acquired from embedded system modules. Through guided lab sessions, learners will configure and test peripherals such as GPIO, ADC, timers, communication interfaces (SPI, I2C, UART), and integrate them into functional applications. - The course highlights real-world practices, including debugging, measurement, documentation of experiments, and the use of development tools such as S32 Design Studio and SDK. By the end of the module, students will gain practical skills in setting up embedded systems, verifying their operation, and applying learned theories to build and demonstrate complete embedded applications.
