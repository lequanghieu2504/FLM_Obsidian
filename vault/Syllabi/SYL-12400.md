## Description
This is a 3-credit course. This course has two parts: online and offline. The content includes basic concepts and applications of IoT and practical exercises on learning KIT. Students are taught how to learn online and practice some parts at home. Q&A sessions, Guidance for important issues, and performance assessments will be conducted in the classroom.
