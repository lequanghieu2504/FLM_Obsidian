## Description
This course offers a comprehensive, yet approachable, guide to public relations plans for students. The knowledge and techniques presented and discussed in this text serve as an effective guide for enhancing students' public relations planning skills. Course objectives: Master the fundamental strategies, tactics, and tools of public relations and those who wish to learn them in the context of the rapidly changing entertainment industry: - Exhibit an advanced understanding of the relationship between entertainment and communication. - Exhibit an advanced understanding of the theories of humanities and social science - Plan a publicity campaign, manage public image, leverage the effects of digital media, and write a compelling press release - Construct professional media and communication practices
