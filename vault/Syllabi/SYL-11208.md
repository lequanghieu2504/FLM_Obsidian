## Description
The course provides students with basic knwoledge of smart cities. The course provides the concepts of smart city, the steps to establish a smart city from technology to management policy. Students gain background knowledge and overview of smart cities.

## Learning Outcomes
| No. | CLO Name | CLO Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | CLO1 | Have an overview of smart city from past, present and future |
| 2 | CLO2 | Understand the definition of smart cities, needs, and challenges of smart cities |
| 3 | CLO3 | Understand the properties, structure, and fucntions of each elements for building smart cities |
| 4 | CLO4 | Understand and have knowledge of building a smart cities from zero to fully functions |
| 5 | CLO5 | Understand and have knowledge in basis of using technologies to build smart cities |
| 6 | CLO6 | Evaluate the different technologies to apply to each specific case and environment for smart cities's applications |
| 7 | CLO7 | Selecting and applying IOT/ICT technologies for building smart cities |
| 8 | CLO8 | Ability to do small project using the different technologies of IoT |
