## Description
Overview of the event industry including conventions, expositions, and major national and international events and their operational requirements.

## Learning Outcomes
| No. | CLO Name | CLO Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | CLO1 | Identify the key characteristics of events, events industry bodies, in order to understand the business of events management as a service activity |
| 2 | CLO2 | Understand the scope and scale of the events business and the determinants of demand for events |
| 3 | CLO3 | Identify the social, economic, political impact and developmental implications of running events |
| 4 | CLO4 | Understand the planning process for events, including the budgetary organization of events, marketing, catering and technical provisions. |
| 5 | CLO5 | Identify issues of risk management in terms of event planning, operations and the issues involved in closing down an event |
| 6 | CLO6 | Intergrate a theoratical framework in to an event project |
