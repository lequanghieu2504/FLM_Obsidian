## Description
The course is Designed to enhance the practical ability of Students. In each session, There are 2 parts of the lecture will be delivered. The theoretical part, given in the form of short-slide, which only focus on the introduction and the key-ideas. The paractical part are given in form of the classworks (exercise in class) will help students understand the introduced topic by a practical approach. During the course, students will learn based on an assignment (given in the form of individual project). Student will decide the topic of the assignment under the supervision of the lecturer. The classworks provide the basic knowledge for the students. Students must investigate them-self and discuss with lecturer to complete the assignment. Beside the assignment (here we consider as long-homework) , there are few given workshops (short-homework). The workshops aim to help the lecturer to evaluate the learning progress of students. Based on that stackholders can recognize the shortcomings of skills and knowledge of the students.
