## Description
This course strengthens learners’ communicative competence by expanding grammar, vocabulary, and cultural understanding for effective everyday and social interaction. Students develop skills to understand extended dialogues and short texts, express opinions and experiences clearly, and write organized paragraphs with accuracy. The course integrates information technology and AI tools to support autonomous and ethical learning. Learners practice collaboration, self-assessment, and reflective strategies to enhance English proficiency and lifelong learning for global communication.
