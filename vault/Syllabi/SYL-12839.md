## Description
This course is designed to develop students' creative writing abilities by exploring the rich landscape of fictional literature. It focuses on the art of storytelling and guides students through the process of character development, plot structuring, setting creation, and narrative techniques through the analysis of influential works. By the end of the course, students will have the opportunity to craft their own fictional stories, incorporating elements learned from both classic and contemporary literature.
