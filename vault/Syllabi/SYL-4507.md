## Description
This course provides students with knowledge of visual arts principles By the end of this course, students will be able to: 1. Understand basic design and visual rules to lay out the composition of art works 2. Analyze, evaluate the effect of design elements in each composition 4. Understand and apply 5 rules of design principles on contemporary artworks (Unity & Harmony, Balance, Scale & Proportion, Contrast & Emphasis, Rhythm) 4. Define the beauty of nature and analyze how it inspires designers to art works 5. Apply visual principles in doing excersises and making art works during career 6. Use drawing tools more effectively with both hand drawing and digital artworks. 6. Plan and organize visual products in the most effective way
