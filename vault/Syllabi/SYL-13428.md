## Description
Description: This course takes you through the key principles of animation, which covers the '12 Principles of Animations' including: Squash & Stretch, Timing & Spacing, Solid Drawing, Anticipation, Follow Through & Overlapping Action, Arcs, Slow in & Slow out, Straight Ahead & Pose to Pose Animation, Secondary Action, Staging, Exaggeration, Appeal. This course will also provides a series of accompanying exercises to reinforce the knowledge of animation principles, including stop-motions, timelapse, flip books, etc... Objective: · Understand the 12 principles of Animations · Know how to apply the 12 principles of motion to a variety of fields including 2D Animation, 3D Animation, Interactive Media, Communication Design. · Complete an animation flipbook or paperless animation. · Complete a short Pixilation clip. · Complete a stop-motion clip
