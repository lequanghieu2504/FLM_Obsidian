## Description
This course facilitates students' comprehension of the impact of transmedia storytelling on engaging and influencing audiences. Students will gain expertise in creating, distributing, and participating in cohesive stories across various traditional and digital channels—for purposes including entertainment, marketing, and social activism. Ultimately, learners will proficiently create and communicate their own transmedia narratives across diverse media channels.
