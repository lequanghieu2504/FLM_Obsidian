## Description
Main objective: The course aims for equipping students hands-on experiences as real front office employees through handle variety of tasks in each position under front office department. Description: Taking school laboratory with standard of 3-star hotel as a real working environment, Front office management 2 focuses particularly on training practical skills in front office deparment for students. The course allows student to experience detailed daily tasks of each position cross front office department such as receptionist, phone operator, cashier, night auditor…etc…With various day to day situation to deal and to handle, learner will be familiar with front office operations enhancing competitive advantage on the hotel job market. -Teaching method: demonstration, roleplay
