## Description
Description: · This course will focus on facilitating the student’s production of 2D animated project. · Idea development, test, problem solving, plan development and time manage will be applied with the selection of appropriate techniques, content and types of 2D animation. · Students work directly with lecturer, with proper explanation and instruction to develop every stage of the project. Objective: · To get used to the implementation of Capstone project. · To have thorough understanding of the whole process to develop short animation from idea to screen showing stage. · To develop skills to produce a completed 2D animation. · To develop a short animation project with proper time management to complete by deadline.
