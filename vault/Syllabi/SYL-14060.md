## Description
This course offers an introduction to the fundamental principles of PR writing, with a specific focus on the influence of integrating artificial intelligence (AI). In the digital age, PR writing has evolved, using diverse channels such as blogs and social media. This evolution surpasses traditional press releases by incorporating the real-time nature of tweets, Instagram stories, LinkedIn posts, and the like. These versatile technologies enable a broad spectrum of approaches to PR writing and engagement, illustrating a noteworthy advancement in public relations techniques that are adept at navigating an ever-evolving communication environment. Upon successful completion of this course, students will acquire: 1. A comprehensive understanding of the transformative impact of digital spaces on information flow and knowledge acquisition, including the ability to distinguish between linear and non-linear media. 2. Proficiency in employing digital writing techniques to enhance readability and user engagement. 3. Mastery in editing for digital media to ensure accuracy and clarity, while adapting to the evolving roles of digital editors and content producers. 4. Ability to strategically utilize multimedia storytelling and public relations techniques, address legal considerations, and combat misinformation to maintain credibility and trust in digital journalism.
