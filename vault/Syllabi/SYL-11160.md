## Description
This course aims to introduce students to understand the basic of technical analysis and how to apply it to investment. After the class, students have ability to use technical analysis to evaluate and predict the future price movement of financial assets, such as stocks, currencies, commodities, by analyzing historical price chart and trading volumes instead of focusing on the intrinsic value of an asset. The course includes the following main topics: Charts and Parttens, Indicators, Candlestick Analysis, Market trends, Risk management.
