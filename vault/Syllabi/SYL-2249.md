## Description
'-Objective: Understand and can be applied the cognitive psychology theories of mass comunication. -Description: This course will focus on a range of psychological theories, processes, and principles in the context of mass communication. Students will explore the application of these theories and principles to several prominent issues will be discussed. Such issues may include politics, sex, and violence, to lesser-studied topics, such as sports, music, emotion, and technology of various media. In addition, this course examines how our experiences with media affect the way we acquire knowledge about the world, and how this knowledge influences our attitudes and behavior. -Teaching method: Presentation, Disscussion, teamwork, critical thinking.
