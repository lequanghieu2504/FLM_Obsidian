## Description
This course provides students with fundamental knowledge of human body structure and proportions, as well as skills in drawing and sketching human figures. It is a foundational course for quick sketching and animation design. The course is offered in conjunction with Coursera's "Anatomy for the Character Design Artist". Throughout the course, students will learn about the history of anatomy, study the structure of the human body in motion, and learn how to draw the proportions of the head, shoulders and arms, legs and feet, and torso. Students will then apply these skills to practice drawing humans in various poses. Finally, students will be able to create character designs that maintain harmonious and visually pleasing human anatomy proportions. In addition, students will be introduced to the use of AI tools in supporting reference generation, pose exploration, and character design ideation, helping them enhance their visual outcomes while still maintaining core anatomy principles.
