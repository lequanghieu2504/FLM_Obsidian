## Description
1. Course Objectives: This course aims to provide learners with a comprehensive understanding of how technology is applied within modern banking systems. Students will explore the historical development of banking technology, key digital infrastructures, and core banking functions such as payments and customer interaction. The course also examines opportunities and risks arising from digital transformation in the financial sector. By the end of the course, learners will be able to analyze banking technology ecosystems and engage effectively with both technical and business stakeholders in the industry. 2. Course Content: The course is organised in six blocks. (1) Foundations of digital banking, including the evolution of banking and its technology landscape; (2) Architecture and data, covering core banking systems, databases, and integration technologies; (3) Digital channels, such as internet/mobile banking and customer interaction across platforms; (4) Payments and operations, including card payments, interbank systems, and risk management technologies; (5) Regulation and governance, focusing on compliance, data protection, and technology management; and (6) Future trends in digital banking, supported by an applied project. 3. Activities and Teaching Methodology: Of the 60 sessions, roughly 40 are guided lectures and case discussions and the remainder are applied workshops, project checkpoints, assessment and review. Workshops ask students to trace transactions end-to-end, read a public banking API portal, audit a real bank's cross-channel journey, map an instant payment flow, and translate a regulatory requirement into technology requirements. All technical content is delivered at conceptual and interpretive level: students read, explain and evaluate architectures and process flows; no programming, configuration or systems administration is required.

## Learning Outcomes
| No. | CLO Name | CLO Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | CLO1 | Describe the role of technology in the banking and financial services business, including banking products and services in the digital era, the technology inheritance, digital-first banking models and the current banking-fintech landscape. |
| 2 | CLO2 | Explain how a bank works as a technology system, including the core banking platform, database and platform architecture, availability and resilience, single customer view, integration and banking APIs, microservices, and banking data and reporting. |
| 3 | CLO3 | Analyse digital banking channels and customer journeys - internet and mobile banking, authentication and digital onboarding, branch, ATM and contact centre - against consistency, availability, security and accessibility. |
| 4 | CLO4 | Analyse payment and banking-operations technologies, including card payments, clearing and settlement, interbank payment schemes, payment platform technology, credit operations and fraud detection, and interpret their operational, cost and risk implications. |
| 5 | CLO5 | Evaluate the regulatory, compliance and governance environment of banking technology - payment and data-protection regulation, Open Banking, KYC/AML obligations, operational resilience, the technology function and sourcing - and its impact on technology decisions. |
| 6 | CLO6 | Develop and present, individually and in teams, a feasible technology-enabled solution to a digital banking or fintech problem, evaluating risks, constraints and the responsible use of data. |
