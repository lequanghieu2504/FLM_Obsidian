## Description
This course examines innovation in stardom, celebrity culture, and social media influence. It explores both tangible and intangible impacts, from disruptive technologies to business model innovation. Students will analyze the evolving dynamics of fame and the interactions that sustain it. The course also investigates the relationship between art and commerce amid digital transformation and the processes and challenges of celebrification faced by traditional celebrities in the digital marketing era.
