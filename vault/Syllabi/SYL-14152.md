## Description
This course introduces essential concepts and methodologies for CMOS analog integrated circuit design, geared toward electronics engineering students with backgrounds in mathematics, physics, circuit theory, electronics, and signal processing. Emphasizing the integration of analytical and simulation approaches, the course utilizes SPICE simulations to illustrate and verify theoretical concepts, providing a practical approach to accurate circuit design. Key topics include MOS transistor modeling, basic gain stages, multi-stage amplifiers, and feedback theory, all foundational to understanding and designing CMOS circuits. The course also covers two-stage operational amplifiers and biasing circuits, with stability and feedback considerations for practical applications. It concludes with a summary of the essential equations and principles, offering students a comprehensive reference for CMOS analog design.
