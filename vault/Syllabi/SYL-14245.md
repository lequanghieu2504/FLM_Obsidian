## Description
Air Cargo Management is a practice‑oriented course that equips you with the knowledge and skills to manage the full life cycle of air freight operations. It focuses on how airlines, freight forwarders, ground handlers, and shippers work together to move high‑value cargo safely, quickly, and profitably in today’s global supply chains. Across 11 chapters you will explore industry structure and regulations, the role of IATA cargo agents and forwarders, aircraft and ULD characteristics, airport and terminal facilities, cargo handling processes, booking and acceptance procedures, and the management of special cargo such as perishables and dangerous goods. You will also learn how to calculate air cargo rates and charges, complete and interpret airway bills, and understand the growing importance of cargo digitalization and automation. Throughout the course, real cases and examples will illustrate how air cargo supports trade and logistics development in Vietnam, helping you see clear links between theory and local industry practice. By the end of the module, you will be able to design and evaluate air cargo solutions, communicate effectively with international partners, and position yourself for careers with airlines, 3PLs, e‑commerce logistics providers, and airport operators.
