## Description
-Objective: To prepare and pratice beer and wine service techniques. -Description: The course focus on practical activities of wines, beers and mixology beverages. -Teaching method: demonstration, roleplay, group work

## Learning Outcomes
| No. | CLO Name | CLO Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | CLO1 | Develop general knowledge on the origins and development of the beverage industry. |
| 2 | CLO2 | Indicate the effects of alcohol in the human body, both positive and negative, from health claims to the latest hangover research. |
| 3 | CLO3 | Develop an overall concept and tailor it to meet the needs and desires of a particular clientele. |
| 4 | CLO4 | Identify and distinguish types of glassware, mixing tools and equipment specifications and uses |
| 5 | CLO5 | Distinguish between fermented and distilled beverages and identify them on your shelves. |
| 6 | CLO6 | Classify wines according to type and recognize some of their distinguishing characteristics. |
| 7 | CLO7 | Prepare and pratice opening techniques of wine bottle and wine service techniques. |
| 8 | CLO8 | Outline the origins of beer and the market trends affecting today’s brewing industry. |
| 9 | CLO9 | Develop a sense of good discipline, a sense of responsibility, and a sense of serious work in the assigned work as well as with colleagues. |
| 10 | CLO10 | Develop ability to work quietly; with cooperation; patience, carefulness, cleanliness and aesthetic values. |
