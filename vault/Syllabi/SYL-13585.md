## Description
This digital painting course equips students with the foundational knowledge and skills to create original artworks in Adobe Photoshop, applying form, light, color, composition, and material rendering through iterative studio practice and critique. AI-powered tools may be used for ideation, composition, and refinement, while the final artwork is executed through original manual painting.

## Learning Outcomes
| No. | CLO Name | CLO Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | CLO1 | Recognise the use of basic tools and techniques of Adobe Photoshop software for digital painting. |
| 2 | CLO2 | Explain the principles of art forms, color theory, light and composition as they apply to digital painting. |
| 3 | CLO3 | Execute textures and materials in digital paintings to enhance realism. |
| 4 | CLO4 | Apply basic and advanced digital painting techniques, integrating AI-powered tools for ideation, composition, and refinement, to create original artworks — including still life, portraits, and landscapes. |
| 5 | CLO5 | Design and create a comprehensive digital painting project that incorporates composition, lighting, color, and texture, using traditional digital painting skills with AI tools as aids for ideation, composition, and refinement. |
| 6 | CLO6 | Demonstrate a professional and positive attitude toward the creative process in digital painting. |
