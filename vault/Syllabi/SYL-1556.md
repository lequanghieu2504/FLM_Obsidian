## Description
The main objective of this course is to help students acquire the vocabulary range and to enhance all language skills required for the advanced level. Additional writing and grammar lessons will be incorporation in the course.

## Learning Outcomes
| No. | CLO Name | CLO Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | CLO1 | Demonstrate knowledge of a vocabulary range covered in lessons (including meaning, opposites, spelling and pronunciation). |
| 2 | CLO2 | Speak confidently and use acceptable expressions in daily communication. |
| 3 | CLO3 | Listen and respond (orally and in writing) to each other, recorded texts and video clips |
| 4 | CLO4 | Show understanding of the reading texts by responding to questions |
| 5 | CLO5 | Respond to writing exercises in the textbook appropriately |
| 6 | CLO6 | Demonstrate understanding of grammar rules taught through exercises. |
| 7 | CLO7 | Show understanding of new words and idioms learnt in textbook |
| 8 | CLO8 | Know of meaning of synonyms, antonyms and homonyms |
| 9 | CLO9 | Write simple, compound and complex sentences correctly. |
| 10 | CLO10 | Know of the role of conjunctions, articles and prepositions in writing |
| 11 | CLO11 | Understand all the tenses system |
| 12 | CLO12 | At the end of the course, write an argumentative essay based on the the studied topics . |
