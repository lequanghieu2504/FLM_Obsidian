## Description
Forming research orientation in the project development process: specifically in the culture and commerce topics. Applying the essential skills for the final project. Getting students to apply the knowledge they have learned and researched in the field of advertising communication: brand identity, packaging, information graphics... to come up with products that are practical and highly applicable. - Students must work in a group. The number of members for each group, decided by the Head Academic of campus, could be variable from 2 to 3 students - The project is done in 4 stages with feedback and discussion at each stage. Students are expected to discuss, organise, present and critique their work with a high level of professionalism, and implement research strategies that contribute to the overall finished project 1) Progress on Project (04 reports): 45% - Report 1: 15% - Report 2: 10% - Report 3: 10% - Report 4: 10% Mark of each report must be >= 4/10. 2) Final Documents (By Instructor): 5% Final Documents (By Instructor) >=4 3) Project Defense - Final Documents (By Council): 20%; - Oral Project Defense: 30% Project Defense >=4 4) Final results:100%; Final Result >=5/10 Final Documents and Final Products must be submitted on time.

## Learning Outcomes
| No. | CLO Name | CLO Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | CLO1 | Conduct and analyze in-depth research on topic-related context, including problem definition, cultural relevance, product life cycle, audience scope, and solution orientation, while using appropriate tools and methods. |
| 2 | CLO2 | Identify core users, define their needs based on research, and map user experiences across the product lifecycle. |
| 3 | CLO3 | Develop visually compelling design concepts that address with user needs and ensure consistency using appropriate visual communication tools . |
| 4 | CLO4 | Create and refine design prototypes that visualize key communication strategies, reflect brand coherence, and support user feedback and iteration. |
| 5 | CLO5 | Conduct appropriate testing methods (e.g., user surveys, print tests), demonstrate understanding of product manufacturing and packaging processes, and effectively present the final outcomes. |
| 6 | CLO6 | Apply project planning and collaboration tools (e.g., Trello, Miro) to coordinate design tasks, manage timelines, and document the development and communication process |
| 7 | CLO7 | Produce visual assets for final product that reflect a cohesive design outcome (e.g., logo, style guide) and a well-structured, style-consistent thesis, oral presentation and defend with clarity and professionalism. |
| 8 | CLO8 | Use AI tools in design research and production ethically and effectively, including paraphrasing, content generation, and verification while maintaining academic integrity. |
