## Description
The course demonstrates concepts, principles, techniques, and applications of ERP systems in general and SAP in particular. The course recalls the components and the structure of an enterprise and introduces the design of SAP that is compatible with this structure. The course also introduces the first three SAP modules, SD (Sales Distribution), MM (Material Management) and PP (Product Planning). Theory, case study, practical sessions and workshop with SAP Experts are arranged alternately, helping students understand the operation of these modules.

| 5 | CLO5 | Evaluate all the tasks performed to solve the case studies |
