## Description
Description: Sketching is an art form that has been used for centuries, the results ranging from the quickest, loosest drawing to much more fully formed and detailed art. It continues to thrive today, being a versatile, portable technique that can be practiced not only at home or in the studio but out and about, while travelling or just gong about our daily lives. Sketching for its own sake can be a relaxing hobby, allowing the artist to practice mindfullness in a fast-paced world. It is also a medium through which we can engage with our world. observation in order to sketch is in itself rewarding, helping us to understand our place in the world and make sense of our surroundings. Sketching encourages us to take an interest in the world, and process what we find in a way that inspires us and ignites the imaginations of others. - The subject will be deployed in the studio with all requirements for learning tools for the subject. Some hours students will practice sketching outside - Speed Drawing subject helps students with Sketch knowledge and skills. This is the most radical recording method of visual arts, helping learners grasp the features, generalize and condense a vivid worldview. - Through the system from basic theory to practical exercises from basic drawing lines to create materials to sketching exercises with human body with strokes, arrays, combination lines, from sketches conceptual design to help students improve knowledge and skills.
