## Description
The course provides foundational knowledge and hands-on experience in connecting, programming, and controlling peripheral devices in modern embedded and IoT systems. Students will explore hardware architecture, communication standards, driver programming techniques, signal processing, and methods for integrating sensors, actuators, and communication modules with microcontrollers or processors. The course focuses on the interaction between hardware, firmware, and peripheral devices, aiming to equip students with the ability to build practical applications such as mini robots, automated control systems, smart IoT devices, and industrial embedded systems.

## Learning Outcomes
| No. | CLO Name | CLO Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | CLO1 | Understanding and Analysis: Students can describe and explain the architecture of embedded systems, the operating principles of peripheral devices, and commonly used communication standards in control systems. |
| 2 | CLO2 | Hardware Design and Interfacing: Students can design and implement connection circuits between microcontrollers and peripheral devices (sensors, actuators, communication modules), ensuring correct voltage levels, signals, and safety requirements. |
| 3 | CLO3 | Communication and Control Programming: Students can write and implement firmware/drivers to communicate with peripheral devices (UART, SPI, I2C, ADC, PWM, etc.) and control actuators in real-world conditions. |
| 4 | CLO4 | System Integration and Problem Solving: Students can integrate multiple peripherals into a complete system, detect, diagnose, and resolve issues related to communication, synchronization, and performance. |
| 5 | CLO5 | Teamwork & Technical Presentation: Students can work effectively in teams and deliver technical reports on the design, interfacing, and control of peripheral devices in labs or project settings. |
