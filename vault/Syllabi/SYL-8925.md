## Description
Based on the study results of Korean language and specialized subjects in the previous semesters, students shall complete a Graduation project with the content and format appropriate to the research area to meet all course requirements. Thus, students are equipped with synthesis knowledge and skills associated with practice and theory about Korean and the subjects designed in the Program to build and implement the Project graduate. Objectives of the course: 1) Help students consolidate and improve their ability to practice Korean in many aspects with specialized knowledge of each selected topic and exploit actual data in Korean to build a successful project. 2) Make Korean effective tool for practical communication with the social environment. 3) Equip the ability to build and complete a project for an idea of ​​the group, and practice teamwork skills. 4) Practice skills in judgment, analysis, selection and problem mining, data mining, technology-related knowledge, etc., to serve each type of project related to each selected topic array. 5) With the final report on the project implementation, students also improve their ability to express themselves in English to present their ideas and working methods, present results, and use documents from a scientific perspective. Hence, they can pursue self-learning even after graduation and nurture a capability of expressing their own thoughts and ideas. The Graduation project is conducted in GROUPS including 04 students (in case the students cannot be divided evenly, there can be a group of 3 or 5 students) The instruction on the Graduation project is organized in two ways: in-class and in separate groups. The in-class instruction is conducted in the first one week with the content regarding the rules, regulations, and things to be noticed during the process of writing the Graduation project. From week 2 onwards, the groups shall meet with the Project supervisor separately to discuss more in-depth about the group's topic. The discussion with the Project supervisor can take place in many different forms appropriate to the availability of the supervisor and the students (meeting in class, discussion via Google Meet and email etc.). The content shall be recorded in the form of reports (based on the forms provided). Thesis topic: the students can choose their topic in one of the following directions: (1) Specialized Korean (3) Korean in Economics and Trade (4) Issues in applied linguistics (5) Issues in Korean language teaching and learning
