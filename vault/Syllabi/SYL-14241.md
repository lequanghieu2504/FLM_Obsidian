## Description
This course provides a comprehensive overview of procurement and supply chain management activities in an international context. Through thematic modules, learners will gain knowledge of global procurement risks, the organizational structures of procurement in multinational corporations, global procurement operations, and the application of information systems in procurement management. The course also explores supplier selection and evaluation strategies, strategic cost management, supply chain analysis techniques, and negotiation and conflict management skills. Additionally, it helps learners understand how to build and maintain global strategic alliances with suppliers to optimize competitive advantage. Moreover, in response to the rapid digital transformation in the industry, the course places a special emphasis on integrating in-depth knowledge of modern technologies applied in practical procurement management. With a strong focus on both theory and practice, this course serves as a solid foundation for developing decision-making and supply chain management capabilities—particularly in formulating effective procurement strategies in the context of globalization.
