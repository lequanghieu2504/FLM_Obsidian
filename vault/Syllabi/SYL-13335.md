## Description
This subject is designed to provide Information Technology (IT) students at FPT University with key activities of digital transformation across the following areas: Data Management and Information Security, Education and Digital Human Resource Development, Project Governance and Sustaining Innovation in Digital Transformation, Law and Ethics in Digital Technology. This knowledge and these skills will help students gain a broader understanding of essential components of digital transformation, preparing them to readily apply their specialized IT knowledge to real-world digital transformation projects within organizations and enterprises, and to become the young, pioneering workforce in Vietnam's Digital Transformation in the near future.

## Learning Outcomes
| No. | CLO Name | CLO Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | CLO1 | Develop the ability to manage data effectively and apply fundamental principles of information security to design, implement, and evaluate solutions that enhance organizational performance and safeguard digital assets. |
| 2 | CLO2 | Provide learners with essential digital knowledge, skills, and mindset to adapt, work effectively, contribute to the development of digital human resources, and promote digital transformation in education |
| 3 | CLO3 | Understand the concepts, characteristics, roles, and core competencies in managing digital transformation projects, along with common challenges. Master modern management methods (Waterfall, Agile, Scrum, Hybrid) and the Scrum process for effective application in digital transformation. Develop robust planning, risk management, organizational structure, and performance measurement using KPIs, OKRs, and the 5-pillar framework. Sustain continuous innovation, build flexible organizations, and create sustainable digital strategies based on PDCA/Kaizen principles and digital maturity levels. |
| 4 | CLO4 | Build the necessary knowledge and skills and the ability to analyze and apply legal regulations and ethical principles to make responsible and lawful decisions, contributing to a safe and sustainable digital environment |
