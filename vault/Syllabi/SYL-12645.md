## Description
The skills taught in this Specialization will empower you to succeed in any college-level course or professional field. You’ll learn to conduct rigorous academic research and to express your ideas clearly in an academic format. In the final Capstone Project, all the knowledge that you’ve gained over the span of these courses will culminate into an academic research paper on an issue of your choice.

## Learning Outcomes
| No. | CLO Name | CLO Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | CLO1 | After completing this course, students will be able to: - identify the correct verb tenses to use - use commas effectively - utilize several different sentence types - write more effectively in English |
| 2 | CLO2 | After completing this course, students will be able to: - create effective thesis statements for essays - plan and write compare/contrast, cause/effect, and argument essays - write well-developed body paragraphs |
| 3 | CLO3 | After completing this course, students will be able to: - plan and write a more sophisticated argument essay - identify plagiarism and explain how to prevent it - read and analyze several articles to form students' own opinion on a topic - make connections between several articles - form thesis statements from students' readings - use sources effectively when writing an essay |
| 4 | CLO4 | After completing this course, students will be able to: - choose appropriate research topics for college classes - write detailed outlines for research papers - find and evaluate reliable sources for research papers - take notes for research - use academic style and tone - document sources by creating a Works Cited list in MLA format - avoid plagiarism |
| 5 | CLO5 | In this capstone project, students will: - conduct research on an academic topic of their own choice - create an outline for the research paper - write a short annotated bibliography - write a 7-8 page research paper - use MLA In-text Citations and MLA Works Cited Page correctly |
