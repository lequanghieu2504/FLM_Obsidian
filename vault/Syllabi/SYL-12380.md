## Description
This course is designed to provide students with a comprehensive understanding of the entire customs process and practice in international trade. It is suitable for anyone who needs to know more about the processes involved in getting your goods through customs smoothly and efficiently. AI Tools will be used to support students in (1) generating ideas/structures for their speaking notes and slide structure of important topics of the course. (2) editing the slide set after students have already finished it.
