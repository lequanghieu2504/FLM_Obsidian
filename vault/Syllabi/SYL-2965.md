## Description
After taking the “basic courses” to get an overview of event organization, students will get the deeper knowledge in planning and running a Tourism and Hospitality Event such as a Thematic Tour, an artistic show, an exhibition, a specific performance, etc. Students see themselves as event operators and they are being required to keep their mind on all relevant elements of an event. Thus, they need expertise and skills in a diverse range of disciplines and nationally, there is a shortage of relevant skills. This course offers opportunities for students to gain valuable skills that make them highly employable in the Touristic Event organization market, and allows them to contribute to a much needed and fast changing professional area. Students work in groups to brainstorm business ideas, then develop a business plan, implement this plan within a semester period. The results of this project will be presented in front of an examiner panel.
