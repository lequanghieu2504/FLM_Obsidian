## Description
This course introduces students to the foundational theories and pressing issues in intercultural communication, with a particular focus on how culture and identity shape human interaction. Through a thematic approach and the use of authentic materials, students engage with real-world examples of communication across cultural boundaries—learning not only how meaning is constructed, but also how it can be misunderstood or transformed in diverse contexts. As the course progresses, students build a solid understanding of how cultural frameworks influence perception, behavior, and dialogue. They develop the ability to communicate effectively and ethically across cultures, learning to navigate both verbal and nonverbal cues with sensitivity. Special emphasis is placed on the responsible use of digital tools and AI technologies to support communication and solve problems in multicultural environments. Beyond knowledge and skills, the course fosters a mindset of openness and respect. Students are encouraged to reflect critically on their own cultural assumptions, embrace diversity, and approach global interactions with empathy and integrity. By the end of the course, they are not only better communicators, but also more thoughtful participants in an increasingly interconnected world.
