## Description
The course helps to build and develop the translation skills for 3rd year students so that at the end of the course learners will be well-versed in the English-Vietnamese and Vietnamese-English translation. Students are expected to acquire and apply basic knowledge of theoretical aspects covering from translation methods, translating process, equivalence, and cultural issues... in their translation. By applying the basic theoretical knowledge, students are expected to be able to efficiently handle compiled jobs in a classroom hypothetical environment, maintain and develop basic English language skills in accordance with translation of 4 main types of texts (C1 standard) including: narrative texts,descriptiom texts, discussion texts, and dialogues.

## Learning Outcomes
| No. | CLO Name | CLO Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | CLO1 | Acquire basic knowledge of translation and its history, the application of theoretical research into translating and editing practice |
| 2 | CLO2 | Acquire knowledge and skills of analyzing a text in translation. Be able to apply the theory of text analysis to translation. |
| 3 | CLO3 | Acquire knowledge of translating process. Be able to apply the process to the translation of Vietnamese into English and vice-versa. |
| 4 | CLO4 | Acquire knowledge of language functions, text categories, types of texts. Be able to apply the theory to the translation of narrative texts (Vietnamese into English and vice-versa) |
| 5 | CLO5 | Acquire knowledge of different translation methods: (Word-for-word translation, literal translation, adaptation, free translation). Be able to apply different methods to translation of description texts (Vietnamese into English and vice-versa) |
| 6 | CLO6 | Acquire knowledge of different translation methods (Semantic & Communicative translation). Be able to use different methods to English-Vietnamese translation of discussion texts. |
| 7 | CLO7 | Acquire basic translation strategies in dealing with non-equivalence at word level, with idioms and fixed expressions, with cove, number, and person. Be able to apply these strategies to translation of idioms, fixed expressions, dialogues. (Vietnamese-English and vice-versa). |
| 8 | CLO8 | Acquire knowledge of cultural issues in translation. Know how to deal with cultural issues to translation. Be able to apply the theory to the translation of related texts. |
| 9 | CLO9 | Have a professional working attitude and have ability to work in a team. |
