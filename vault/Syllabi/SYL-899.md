## Description
The course covers the basic knowledge neccessary for transmitting and receiving information using today's communication technologies. It is the introduction to communication systems: analogue vs. digital communications, analogue modulation techniques (amplitude modulation, frequency modulation, phase modulation), broadcast radio and TV systems, source coding, communication channels (noise, bandwidth), signalling over bandlimited channels (matched filter, intersymbol interference (ISI), Nyquist criterion), binary digital modulation techniques (amplitude shift keying, frequency shift keying, phase shift keying). Topics covered: Introduction; Fourier Representation of Signals and Systems; Amplitude Modulation; Angle Modulation; Pulse Modulation: Transition from Analog to Digital Communications; Baseband Data Transmission; Digital Band-Pass Modulation Techniques; Random Signals and Noise; Noise in Analog Communications; Noise in Digital Communications; System and Noise Calculations
