## Description
This course introduces students to the fundamentals of book design and printing technology. Students will learn about book anatomy, layout principles, typography, image integration, and basic binding techniques. Emphasis is placed on audience-centered design, effective visual communication, and hands-on experimentation with materials. Objectives: Upon completion of this course, students will be able to: - Design comprehensive book layouts, including covers, interior pages, and structural elements. - Select and apply appropriate typefaces, sizes, and visual elements to enhance readability and engagement. - Integrate illustrations and hands-on application of materials effectively within book layouts. - Demonstrate proficiency in basic bookbinding techniques. - Adapt design choices to suit specific audiences and contexts.
