## Description
This course explores how media technologies evolve, function, and impact society, helping students think critically about media and culture. It introduces the global media landscape and the ongoing shifts in technology, culture, and critical theory. Emphasis is placed on how new media shape power relations and social connections through communication. Topics include social media trends, digital publishing, Internet governance, privacy protection, online advertising, and the video game industry. Students will engage with real-world issues in a fast-changing media environment.
