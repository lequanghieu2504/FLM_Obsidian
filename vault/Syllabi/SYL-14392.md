## Description
This course provides a comprehensive introduction to digital marketing, with a particular focus on major social media platforms such as Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube, TikTok, and others. It aims to equip students with the fundamental knowledge, skills, and tools required to build and grow engaging online communities, as well as to strategically develop and successfully execute effective social media advertising campaigns.
