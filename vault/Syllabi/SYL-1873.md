## Description
Objective: 'This course provides English major students with an ingight to some popular ESL teaching methods in the 21st century which aim to develop a mindset of more student-centered approaches. Teaching method: lecture + project work Assessment scheme: On-going assessment: - Assignment 1: 30% - Assignment 2: 30% - Assignment 3: 30% - Oral defense: 10%

## Learning Outcomes
| No. | CLO Name | CLO Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | CLO1 | Identify and explain the alignment of LOs, delivery and assessment in Outcome-based education (OBE) |
| 2 | CLO2 | Construct an ESL course's LOs |
| 3 | CLO3 | Recognize the nature of project-based learning and its role |
| 4 | CLO4 | Sketch a project for ESL learners that can match the LOs |
| 5 | CLO5 | Design the rubrics for the project |
| 6 | CLO6 | Identify the role of assessment in OBE |
| 7 | CLO7 | Compare different kinds of assessment and their purposes |
| 8 | CLO8 | Construct an assessment scheme for an ESL course with different components |
