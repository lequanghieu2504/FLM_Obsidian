## Description
This comprehensive course on Embedded Systems with the S32K144 microcontroller offers a structured journey from foundational concepts to advanced practical applications, tailored for students, engineers, and enthusiasts interested in automotive and real-time embedded technologies. Spanning 20 sessions, the program begins with an overview of embedded systems history, classification, and the S32K144's architecture (based on ARM Cortex-M4), emphasizing its role in automotive applications like ECUs, body control, and powertrain systems. It progresses through key topics such as memory organization, clock management, GPIO, timers, interrupts, pin configuration, debugging, and optimization for performance and power efficiency. Theoretical sessions are complemented by hands-on practical labs, where students implement concepts using tools like S32 Design Studio and SDK. The course culminates in a group project presentation, fostering teamwork and real-world problem-solving skills. By the end, participants will be equipped to design, develop, and optimize embedded systems for constrained environments, with a strong focus on automotive standards, energy efficiency, and reliability.
