## Description
The course focuses on process intelligence gathering and intelligence analysis with target-centric approach method. Describe some ways to increase the effectiveness of the of predict analysis. The course provides a general guide to help students understand the technical intelligence analysis. Assessment scheme: 1. On-going assessment: + Quiz: 4 quizzes, 20% (5% per each) + Project review: 10% + Assignment: 30% + Presentation: 10% 2. Final exam: 30% 3. Total: 100% Evaluation criteria: Completion Criteria: Every on-going assessment component > 0, Final Result >=5 & Final Exam Score >=4
