## Description
This advanced-level course offers students a comprehensive examination of PR writing in both Vietnamese and English. Participants will acquire skills in goal identification, information curation, and the strategic creation and publication of content. The course also explores the symbiotic relationship between PR writing and the dynamics of transmedia storytelling in attracting and influencing audiences. Through practical exercises, students will engage in designing, sharing, and participating in cohesive storytelling experiences across a range of traditional and digital platforms, including entertainment, advertising, marketing, and social change. The curriculum further encompasses the effective application of AI tools to enhance storytelling and transmedia production processes.
