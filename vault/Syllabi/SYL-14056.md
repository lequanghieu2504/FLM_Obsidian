## Description
The course describes the corporation and its operating environment, the manner in which corporate boards and management evaluate investment opportunities and arrangements for financing such investments. It also brings discussion in wide range context under varying conditions of aggregate demand, inflation, tax rates, interest rates, among other variables as well as the tools for valuing the short-term movements in bond and equity valuations in the various stock markets are even more complexly determined. Topics include: - Introduction to corporate finance; - Financial statements and how they are related, as well as how these statements are related to cash fl ows to investors; - Ratio analysis and other tools used to evaluate financial statements; - The Time Value of Money and then applies present value concepts to bond and stock valuation; - The principle tool for evaluating capital projects and alternative capital budgeting decision rules; - How project cash flows are calculated and forecast.
