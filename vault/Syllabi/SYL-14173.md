## Description
This course equips students with foundational and applied knowledge of automotive electrical and electronic systems. It covers the operating principles of engine electrical systems, vehicle body electrical components, and the various electronic features integrated into modern vehicles. Students will explore the structure, functions, and operating mechanisms of Electronic Control Units (ECUs), including how they monitor, regulate, and optimize different vehicle subsystems. Through this course, learners will develop both theoretical understanding and practical insights into the electrical and electronic technologies that underpin contemporary automotive engineering.
