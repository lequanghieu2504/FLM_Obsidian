## Description
The course is designed to enable students to: - develop and deliver compelling presentations. - balance proven ideas with lots of speech practice drawn from the established knowledge in communication, rhetoric, linguistics, and argumentation, each course 1) Ongoing assessment: Student gets 0.5 bonus points for each course completed on time. The total bonus point is not greater than 1. 2) Practical Exam (PE) 3) Final Result (FR) =min (10, PE+ Bonus) 4) Completion Criteria: PE>=4 and FR>=5

## Learning Outcomes
| No. | CLO Name | CLO Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | CLO1 | Design persuasive speeches that address problems and solutions and that motivate audience members; Use rhetorical style strategically and deliver passionate and compelling speeches. |
| 2 | CLO2 | Compose and deliver moving speeches that praise core audience values through evidence, storytelling, and humor; Write eloquent passages in tones appropriate to the audience and occasion and speak fluidly from either a well-prepared manuscript or from memory. |
