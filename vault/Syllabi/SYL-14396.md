## Description
Multimodal AI course aim on building AI systems that operate across modalities - vision, language, audio, video, 3D, and embodiment. The course frames multimodality through four core problems (representation, alignment, fusion, translation) and proceeds through cross-modal representations (CLIP, SigLIP, ImageBind), fusion architectures (cross-attention, Perceiver, Q-Former, gated cross-attention), vision-language and unified models (LLaVA, BLIP-2, Flamingo, Chameleon/Janus families), speech and audio, video and world models, 3D and embodied agents, multimodal reasoning / agents / RAG, evaluation (benchmark saturation, hallucination), efficiency and deployment, and safety/provenance/frontiers. Mathematical depth is assumed from the prerequisites (Deep Learning, Advanced Generative AI); this course focuses on architectures, training recipes, evaluation, and hands-on multimodal systems.

## Learning Outcomes
| No. | CLO Name | CLO Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | CLO1 | Identify and characterize multimodal AI systems and the four core problems of multimodal machine learning - representation, alignment, fusion, and translation. |
| 2 | CLO2 | Build cross-modal representations using contrastive and self-supervised methods, and evaluate them via zero-shot transfer and cross-modal retrieval. |
| 3 | CLO3 | Design and implement fusion architectures (cross-attention, latent bottlenecks, connector modules) that combine heterogeneous modalities. |
| 4 | CLO4 | Apply vision-language and unified multimodal architectures (LLaVA, BLIP-2, Flamingo, and unified token/diffusion-transformer families) and instruction-tune them. |
| 5 | CLO5 | Apply multimodal techniques to non-image modalities - speech, audio, video, 3D, and embodied agents - and to cross-modal generation. |
| 6 | CLO6 | Engineer multimodal reasoning, agent, and retrieval systems, and address the input-size and latency challenges of deploying them. |
| 7 | CLO7 | Evaluate multimodal systems with appropriate benchmarks and protocols, recognizing saturation and the migrating frontier, and run hallucination and grounding diagnostics. |
| 8 | CLO8 | Analyze the safety, provenance, bias, and privacy issues specific to multimodal AI - deepfakes, watermarking, and biometric data. |
