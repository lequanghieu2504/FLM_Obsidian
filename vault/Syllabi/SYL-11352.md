## Description
This introductory course provides an instructional manual to equip students with a theoretical basis for, and essential skills in, Interpretation. Two common types are put into focus; Consecutive Interpreting and Simultaneous Interpreting. The objective is to train students to be capable of interpreting in various fields and settings.
