## Description
This course will describe five primary stages in a multimedia project and determine the scope of a multimedia project. These may include phases, tasks, costs, timelines, and elements of a multimedia project proposal. Students will learn how to apply artificial intelligence (AI) tools and digital technologies to enhance project performance. They will use these tools to optimize content creation, streamline workflows, and improve project management across multimedia projects. In addition, this course gives students opportunities to practice working on a real-life project. The lecturer will use several teaching methods, such as conducting group discussions and sharing fieldwork experience, in addition to regular teaching. Students will have the chance to present their work as well as their thinking process related to the project.

## Learning Outcomes
| No. | CLO Name | CLO Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | CLO1 | Explain the five main stages of a multimedia project, the intangible factors affecting production quality, the key team roles with their required skills, and the common hardware/software tools used. Includes guidance on selecting suitable platforms for effective multimedia production. |
| 2 | CLO2 | Analyze and determine the scope, objectives, target audience, creative strategy, and budget of a multimedia project, examine the intangible elements needed to make good multimedia production |
| 3 | CLO3 | Develop a complete project plan with clear phases, tasks, schedules, resource allocation, and a well-defined project scope. Evaluate project requirements such as cost, timeline, workload, and risks necessary for completing a multimedia project. |
| 4 | CLO4 | Apply multimedia production tools and AI-based tools to optimize workflows and enhance project efficiency. |
| 5 | CLO5 | Evaluate overall quality of the project, delivers the intended message or experience, and fulfills user needs within the defined scope and constraints. |
| 6 | CLO6 | Have accountability, good self-organization, and strong collaborative skills while working with the team on the multimedia project. Contribute actively and communicates effectively to support overall project success. |
