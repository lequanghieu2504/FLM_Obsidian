Syllabus Details

Syllabus ID: 14047
Syllabus Name: Financial Statement Analysis_Phân tích báo cáo tài chính
Course Name English: Financial Statement Analysis
Subject Code: ACC305
Learning-Teaching Method: Lecture,Problem based learning,Presentation,discussion,groupwork
NoCredit: 3
Degree Level: Bachelor
Time Allocation: Study hour (150h) = 45h (60 sessions) contact hours + 1h final exam + 104h self-study
Pre-Requisite: ACC101
Description: The course provides a framework for interpretation, analysis and evaluation of financial statements from viewpoints of creditors, owners, investment firms and others. Thereby, the users of accounting information understand business strengths or weakness, the impact of general business and specific industry situations, behavior of financial markets, credit or lending criteria, and equity investment standards on present and future company financial conditions.
After completing the course, the student shall be able to:
- Be familiar with the organization and disclosure of reported information
- Quantitatively and qualitatively evaluate core accounting issues that are particularly relevant in the context of financial statement analysis.
- Have a framework for forecasting future earnings and financial performance.
- Evaluate the business financial performance by applying quantitative and qualitative techniques.
- Use financial statement information in a modeling environment to identify and recommend solutions to a variety of financial challenges, and to recommend investment decisions.
StudentTasks: - Class attendance is strongly encouraged. Attend at least 80% of class hours in order to be accepted to the final examination
- Actively participate in class activities
- Fulfill tasks given by the instructor after class
- Use laptop in class only for learning purposes
- Read the textbook in advance
- Access the course website (http://flm.fpt.edu.vn) for up-to-date information and material on the course, for online support from teachers and other students, and for practicing and assessment.
