Syllabus ID: 13756
Syllabus Name: Advanced English Grammar_Ngữ pháp tiếng Anh nâng cao
Course Name English: Advanced English Grammar
Subject Code: ENG303
Learning-Teaching Method: Offline
NoCredit: 3
Degree Level: Bachelor
Time Allocation: Study hour (150h) = 45 contact hours (60 sessions) + 104 hours of self-study + 1 hour for the final exam
1 session = 45 minutes
Pre-Requisite: None
Description: The course is designed to consolidate and enhance students' grammatical knowledge. It aims to achieve this by giving learners exposure to grammar as it is used in real life situations, offering them comprehensive explanations for grammatical forms and meanings, providing contextualized practice of various structures, and developing their use of such structures as well as their critical thinking skills through communicative activities. Students will also be informed of how to recognize common grammatical errors in their written work and encouraged to apply learned grammar in writing. Utilizing a flipped classroom approach, they will engage with core grammar concepts and materials independently before class sessions. They are also encouraged to leverage AI tools as resources to support their grammar learning, practice, and writing processes.
StudentTasks: - Students must attend more than 80% of contact slots in order to be accepted to the final examination.
- Students are responsible to do all exercises and assignments given by instructor in class or at home and submit on time
- Use laptops in class only for learning purposes

- Compare and contrast AI’s explanations to advanced grammatical structures when using different prompting techniques
- Compare and contrast AI’s examples when using different prompting techniques

Suggested prompting techniques for the activities:
+ Zero-shot prompting: Give AI a direct, no-example request.
+ Few-shot prompting: Provide a few examples within the prompt to guide AI on the desired format or style.
+ One-shot prompting: A specific type of few-shot prompting where only one example is given.
+ Chain-of-Thought (CoT) prompting: Break down complex problems into smaller, sequential steps to guide the model through a logical process.
+ Contextual prompting: Provide extra background information and details to improve AI's understanding.
