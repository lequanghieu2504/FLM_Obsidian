Syllabus ID: 13459
Syllabus Name: Introduction to International Business_Nhập môn kinh doanh quốc tế
Course Name English: Introduction to International Business
Subject Code: IBI101
Learning-Teaching Method: Lecture,Presentation
NoCredit: 3
Degree Level: Bachelor
Time Allocation: Study hour (150h) = 45h contact hours + 1h final exam + 104h self-study
Pre-Requisite: None
Description: Introduction to International Business is designed to equip students with a comprehensive understanding of the fundamental principles and practices of conducting business in a globalized economy. It explores the dynamic interplay of socio-cultural, economic, technological, and political-legal factors that shape the international business environment. Students will gain insights into the strategies and techniques for entering and operating in foreign markets, including trade policies, global supply chains, and cross-border management. A key feature of this course is the integration of artificial intelligence (AI) tools and applications, enabling students to analyze international market trends, optimize decision-making processes, and develop innovative solutions to real-world business challenges. By the end of the course, students will be prepared to navigate the complexities of international business with a forward-thinking, technology-driven approach.
StudentTasks: - Attend at least 80% of contact hours in order to be accepted to the final examination
- Actively participate in class activities
- Fulfill tasks given by instructor after class
- Use their own laptop in class only for learning purpose
- Read the textbook in advance
- Access the course website (www.flm.fpt.edu.vn) for up-to-date information and material of the course, for online supports from teachers and other students and for practicing and assessment.

No. CLO Name CLO Details
1 CLO1 Recognize the key issues of globalization, and the national differences in political, economic, legal systems and culture
2 CLO2 Demonstrate understanding about the ethical, corporate social responsibility, and sustainability faced by international business, and explaination how global managers can incorporate such issues into their decision making
3 CLO3 Demonstrate understanding about the global trade and investment environment in which international business occurs
4 CLO4 Describe characteristics of the foreign exchange market, different theories relating to exchange rate and how to manage foreign exchange risks
5 CLO5 Identify the different global strategies and entry modes that firms use to compete in the global marketplace
6 CLO6 Explain how firms can perform their key functions including global production, global supply chain management, global marketing, global research and development to compete and succeed in the international business environment
7 CLO7 Evaluate external environment of a specific foreign market
8 CLO8 Propose the suitable entry mode for a specific company to enter foreign market.
9 CLO9 Demonstrate attitude to adapt to differences and complex issues in doing international business
10 CLO10 Apply AI to analyze global market trends and consumer sentiment, optimize solutions, and critically address bias, risks, and ethical responsibilities.
