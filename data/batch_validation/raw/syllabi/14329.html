Syllabus ID: 14329
Syllabus Name: Financial Reporting_Báo cáo tài chính
Course Name English: Financial Reporting
Subject Code: FIN306c
Learning-Teaching Method: Online
NoCredit: 3
Degree Level: Bachelor
Time Allocation: Online 49 hours+ 2 slots offline (optional)+108 hours self study
Pre-Requisite: Passed ACC101
Description: The Financial Reporting Specialization focuses on the role of financial accounting principles and processes in creating and reporting an organization’s financial statements. Learners who complete this specialization will be able to (1) use financial accounting principles to create and/or process an organization’s financial statements and (2) analyze financial statements to assess an organization’s financial position.
Note: Students who complete all MOOCs before the course deadline will receive 1 bonus point; otherwise, no bonus point will be awarded.
StudentTasks: 1. Join the online course (spec.) when being invited by the admin.
2. Enroll in each MOOC of spec., regularly study MOOCs at least 5hrs/week by watching videos, reading materials, taking quizzes, doing assgnments... as required
3. Respect the Coursera Code of Conduct, at https://learner.coursera.help/hc/en-us/articles/208280036-Coursera-Code-of-Conduct
3. Keep online learning progress promptly (bonus will be awarded)
4. Communicate and discuss in forum
5. Attend off-line sessions in the Campus (Optional)
6. Must complete all MOOCs of spec. with certification in order to take the Final Exam at Campus

No. CLO Name CLO Details
1 CLO1 Using financial accounting principles to create and/or process an organization’s financial statements.
2 CLO2 Analyzing financial statements to assess an organization’s financial position.
3 CLO3 Explain the accounting concepts, principles and theory with an emphasis on problems that arise in applying these concepts for external reporting purposes.
4 CLO4 Measuring of assets, liabilities, equities and income, as well as disclosure of additional information that may assist users understand the financial reports.
5 CLO5 Identify the current liabilities and contingencies and continues with long-term debt and bonds.
6 CLO6 Explain the accounting model for leases, both lessees and lessors.
7 CLO7 Explain the deferred tax assets and liabilities.
8 CLO8 Explain the recognition, measurement and subsequent accounting for equity, pensions, share-based compensation.
9 CLO9 Explain the cash flows utilizing the FASB Accounting Standards Codification and other resources
10 CLO10 Ability to combine the concepts and techniques obtained through all the courses in this specialization.
11 CLO11 Apply the concepts and techniques obtained through all the courses in this specialization to a real world accounting project.
12 CLO12 Practising the project
